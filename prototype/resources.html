FAQ

What partners ask most

Do you sell to end users?

The channel model serves resellers, integrators and retailers. End users are routed to our partner network.

How fast are quotes?

RFQs are answered within one business day (Sun–Thu) with volume-tiered pricing.

What warranty applies?

Full manufacturer warranty on all 23 authorized lines, handled locally through the Simal RMA desk.

Do you support Peppol e-invoicing?

Yes — TRN and Peppol ID are captured at onboarding ahead of the 2027 mandate.

Can we resell UniERP?

Partners can earn UniERP (Odoo 19 CE) territory rights with implementation support from UniSoft.

Which regions do you serve?

UAE, GCC, MEA and CIS — fulfilled from JAFZA, with Dhaka covering South Asia.
