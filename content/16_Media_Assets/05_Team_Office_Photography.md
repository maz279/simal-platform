## Headshot Specifications

| Property | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Dimensions** | 800×800px (1:1 square) |
| **Background** | Brand blue gradient or clean office background |
| **Style** | Professional, friendly, consistent lighting |
| **File Naming** | `headshot-{firstname}-{lastname}-800.jpg` |

---

## Office Photography Guidelines

- Natural lighting preferred; supplement with soft artificial light
- No cluttered desks or visible confidential documents
- Include people working naturally (not posed staring at camera)
- Show collaborative spaces and modern equipment
- Dubai skyline visible from windows where possible
