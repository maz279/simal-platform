# 04 Product Image Specifications

> **Category:** Product Photography
> **Storage:** `/images/products/`
> **Formats:** JPG (primary), WebP (optimized)
> **Last Updated:** May 2, 2026

---

## Product Image Standards

| Property | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Background** | Pure white (#FFFFFF) or transparent (PNG only) |
| **Angles Per Product** | Front, back, angled (45°), top, detail/close-up |
| **Minimum Dimensions** | 1200×1200px (1:1 square, main image) |
| **Resolution** | 72 DPI (screen-optimized) |
| **Lighting** | Studio lighting, no harsh shadows, even illumination |
| **Composition** | Product centered, 85-90% of frame, 5% padding all sides |
| **Watermark** | NO WATERMARKS on product images |
| **Consistency** | All products in same category use same angle and lighting style |

---

## Image Variants Per Product

| Variant | Dimensions | File Suffix | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Full Size** | 1200×1200px | `-1200` | Lightbox zoom, detail view |
| **Standard** | 800×800px | `-800` | Product detail page main image |
| **Thumbnail** | 400×400px | `-400` | Product cards, listings, search results |
| **Mini** | 150×150px | `-150` | Cart, order summary, quick view |

## Product Photography Checklist

- [ ] White/transparent background (no desk, no shadows on background)
- [ ] Product is clean and dust-free
- [ ] Labels and text on product are legible
- [ ] No reflections obscuring product details
- [ ] Consistent angle across all products in same category
- [ ] File named correctly: `{brand}-{model}-{angle}-{size}.jpg`
- [ ] Alt text prepared in all 4 languages
