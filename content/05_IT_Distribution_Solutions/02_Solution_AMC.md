## The AMC Advantage

### Predictable IT Costs
Convert unpredictable break-fix expenses into a **fixed annual budget** — no surprise repair bills. AMC covers labor and parts (per your tier), so you always know your IT costs.

### Increased Uptime
Proactive maintenance catches issues before they cause downtime. Our Premium tier clients average **99.9% system availability** — critical for businesses where every minute of downtime costs revenue.

### Extended Hardware Lifespan
Regular maintenance, cleaning, firmware updates, and component replacement extend the useful life of your IT assets by **20–40%**, maximizing your initial hardware investment.

### Access to Certified Engineers
Your AMC gives you priority access to Simal Technologies' team of **certified IT engineers** (Sophos, Microsoft, Cisco, HPE, Dell, VMware) — without the overhead of hiring full-time IT staff.

### Compliance Support
For regulated industries (banking, healthcare, government), our AMC programs include documentation and audit trails that support **ISO 27001, NESA, and GDPR compliance** requirements.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What's the minimum contract duration?**
A: Our standard AMC contracts are 12 months. We also offer multi-year contracts with discounted rates.

**Q: Do you cover equipment not purchased from Simal Technologies?**
A: Yes. Our AMC covers all IT equipment regardless of purchase source, subject to a pre-contract audit.

**Q: What about after-hours support?**
A: Standard and Premium tiers include after-hours support. Basic tier is business hours only.

**Q: Can the AMC be customized?**
A: Absolutely. We tailor scope, SLA levels, and pricing to your specific needs.

**Q: Do you provide loaner equipment during repairs?**
A: Premium tier includes hot-spare replacement. Standard tier offers loaner equipment for critical systems.
