## How We Work — Our Solution Delivery Process

```
┌──────────┐    ┌──────────┐    ┌──────────┐    ┌──────────┐    ┌──────────┐    ┌──────────┐
│ DISCOVER │───▶│  DESIGN  │───▶│ PROPOSE  │───▶│  DEPLOY  │───▶│ MONITOR  │───▶│  SUPPORT │
└──────────┘    └──────────┘    └──────────┘    └──────────┘    └──────────┘    └──────────┘
```

| Phase | Description | Deliverables | Duration |
|-------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| **1. Discover** | We assess your current IT infrastructure, business requirements, pain points, and goals through on-site consultation | Assessment report, gap analysis | 1–3 days |
| **2. Design** | Our solution architects design a tailored solution with specific hardware, software, and service configurations | Solution architecture document, BoM (Bill of Materials) | 3–10 days |
| **3. Propose** | We present a detailed proposal with scope, timeline, pricing, and SLA terms for your review and approval | Formal proposal, SLA agreement, commercial offer | 1–5 days |
| **4. Deploy** | Our certified engineers execute the deployment — installation, configuration, integration, and testing | Fully operational solution, deployment report, user training | Per project scope |
| **5. Monitor** | We implement proactive monitoring to detect and resolve issues before they impact your operations | Real-time dashboard, monthly health reports | Ongoing |
| **6. Support** | Our support team provides ongoing maintenance, updates, and 24/7 helpdesk access per your SLA tier | Regular maintenance, patch management, technical support | Ongoing per contract |
