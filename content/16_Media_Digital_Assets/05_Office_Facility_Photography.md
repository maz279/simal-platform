## 6. Technical Standards (All Facilities)

### 6.1 Resolution & Format

| Variant | Width | Format | Use |
|---------|-------|--------|-----|
| **Full (Archive)** | 4800 px (long edge) | TIFF / RAW | Master archive, print |
| **Web — Standard** | 2400 px (long edge) | WebP + JPEG | Website hero sections, gallery |
| **Web — Retina** | 4800 px (long edge) | WebP + JPEG | HiDPI displays |
| **Thumbnail** | 600 px (long edge) | WebP + JPEG | Gallery thumbnails, cards |

### 6.2 Post-Processing

```
RAW →
  1. Lens correction (distortion, vignette)
  2. White balance (mixed lighting correction)
  3. Exposure blending (HDR if needed for windows/lighting)
  4. Perspective correction (vertical lines straight)
  5. Color grade (consistent across all facility photos)
  6. Crop to delivery aspect ratios
  7. Export WebP + JPEG variants
```

### 6.3 Consistency

- All facility photos share a consistent color grade (slightly warm, natural)
- Vertical lines must be perfectly straight (no keystoning)
- White balance consistent: 5000–5500K target
- No selective color effects or heavy filters

## 8. Shoot Logistics

### 8.1 Schedule

| Location | Best Time | Duration | Preparation |
|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|
| Dubai HQ | Morning (8–11 AM) for interior; late afternoon for exterior | 4 hours | Office tidied; branded elements displayed |
| JAFZA Warehouse | Morning (9 AM–12 PM) | 3 hours | Warehouse organized; safety compliance check |
| Bur Dubai Store | Morning (10 AM–12 PM) — before opening | 2 hours | Store merchandised; clean windows |
| Dhaka Office | Morning (9 AM–12 PM) local time | 3 hours | Office prepared; team briefed |

### 8.2 Pre-Shoot Checklist

- [ ] Facilities notified 1 week in advance
- [ ] Areas to be photographed cleaned and organized
- [ ] Confidential documents / client information removed from visible areas
- [ ] Staff briefed on photo shoot; those not wanting to appear can step aside
- [ ] Branded elements (logos, awards, signage) cleaned and properly positioned
- [ ] Windows cleaned for exterior/interior shots
- [ ] Any repairs/maintenance completed before shoot
- [ ] Backup date identified in case of bad weather (for exterior shots)

### 8.3 Permissions

| Location | Permission Required |
|----------|-------------------|
| Dar Al Riffa Building | Building management; photography permit for commercial use |
| JAFZA (Jebel Ali Free Zone) | JAFZA authority; StarSeed internal approval |
| Bur Dubai Store | Stellent Technologies management; RTA if street visible in shot |
| Dhaka Office | Smart Technologies BD management |
