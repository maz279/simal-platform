## 2. Executive Leadership Headshots

### 2.1 Technical Specifications

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Resolution** | 800 × 800 px (square crop) — web |
| **Retina (2×)** | 1600 × 1600 px |
| **Background** | Neutral gray (#F0F0F0) or brand blue gradient |
| **Lighting** | Soft, even; clamshell lighting (key above, fill below) |
| **Lens** | 85mm f/1.8 or 70-200mm f/2.8 at 100mm+ |
| **Aperture** | f/2.8–f/4.0 (subject sharp, background softly out of focus) |
| **Pose** | Slight angle (15°–20° from camera), shoulders relaxed |
| **Expression** | Professional, approachable, confident smile |
| **Attire** | Business formal (suit/blazer) or business casual (collared shirt) |
| **Format** | WebP + JPEG; square crop |

## 5. Office Culture & Candid Photography

### 5.1 Shot Categories

| Category | Quantity | Settings / Moments |
|----------|----------|--------------------|
| **Daily Work** | 3–4 | Team members at desks, collaborative work, client calls |

| **Social / Culture** | 2–3 | Team lunch, celebration, UAE National Day, Ramadan Iftar |
| **Training** | 1–2 | Staff training session, product demo, knowledge sharing |

### 5.2 Style Guidelines

| Element | Guideline |
|---------|----------|
| **Style** | Documentary / editorial; natural, unstaged feel |
| **Lighting** | Available light preferred; no flash if distracting |
| **Composition** | Rule of thirds, leading lines, natural framing |
| **Candid vs. Posed** | 80% candid, 20% lightly directed |
| **Diversity** | Show age, gender, nationality diversity of Simal's workforce |

## 8. Photo Shoot Logistics

### 8.1 Scheduling

| Session | Content | Duration | Timing |
|---------|---------|----------|--------|
| **Session 1** | Executive headshots (8 people) | 3 hours | Morning (9 AM–12 PM) |
| **Session 2** | Department heads (10 people) | 3 hours | Afternoon (1 PM–4 PM) |
| **Session 3** | Group photos (5 groups) | 2 hours | Morning (9 AM–11 AM) |
| **Session 4** | Office culture / candids | Full day | Throughout workday |
| **Session 5** | B2B & UniERP team | 3 hours | Afternoon |

### 8.2 Equipment Checklist

- [ ] Full-frame camera (×2 for backup)
- [ ] 85mm f/1.8 lens (headshots)
- [ ] 24–70mm f/2.8 lens (groups, candids)
- [ ] 70–200mm f/2.8 lens (candids, compressed shots)
- [ ] Studio strobe ×2 with softboxes/umbrellas
- [ ] Portable backdrop (gray, white)
- [ ] Reflector (5-in-1)
- [ ] Light stands ×2
- [ ] Gray card for white balance
- [ ] Tethering cable + laptop (for instant review)
- [ ] Stool/chair for seated portraits
- [ ] Lint roller, mirror (for touch-ups)
- [ ] Model release forms (printed + digital)

## 9. Consent & Model Release

### 9.1 Model Release Form

All photographed individuals must sign a model release covering:

- **Usage:** Simal Technologies corporate website, social media, print collateral, presentations
- **Duration:** Perpetual (until revoked in writing)
- **Territory:** Worldwide
- **Compensation:** None (voluntary, as employees)
- **Opt-Out:** Employee may request removal at any time by emailing HR

### 9.2 Data Privacy (UAE PDPL Compliance)

- Photos are considered personal data under UAE PDPL (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021)
- Storage: Secure server with access limited to Marketing & HR
- Retention: Active for duration of employment + 2 years after departure
- Removal: Upon written request, photo removed from website within 14 business days

## 10. Accessibility

- **Alt Text:** Every team photo includes descriptive alt text with person's name, title, and context
  - Example: `alt="Ahmed Al-Rashid, Managing Director of Simal Technologies Middle East LLC"`
- **Group Photos:** Include a text-based list of all visible individuals in a collapsible section below the image
- **Decorative Images:** Candid photos used purely for visual enhancement use `alt=""`
