## 1. Overview

Short product demonstration videos showcase flagship products in action, helping B2B buyers visualize real-world use cases and build confidence in purchasing decisions. These 30–60 second clips are embedded on product detail pages and shared across social media.

### 1.1 Video Specifications

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Duration** | 0:30–1:00 per product |
| **Aspect Ratio** | 16:9 (product pages); 1:1 and 9:16 (social) |
| **Resolution** | 1920×1080 (Full HD) |
| **Frame Rate** | 30 fps (standard) or 60 fps (gaming/smooth motion) |
| **Codec** | H.264 (web) |
| **Audio** | Background music only (no voice-over); AAC 256 kbps |
| **Hosting** | YouTube (Simal Product Demos playlist) |

## 5. Shot Style Guide

### 5.1 Visual Approach

| Element | Guideline |
|---------|----------|
| **Lighting** | Bright, clean, product-forward; ring light fill for detail shots |
| **Background** | Dark gray or gradient (#1A1A2E to #005B9F) — consistent across all videos |
| **Camera Movement** | Smooth gimbal/dolly slides; avoid handheld shake |
| **Transitions** | Clean cuts + occasional smooth whip-pans between features |
| **Text Overlays** | Inter font, white with subtle drop shadow, animated fade/slide in |

### 5.2 Music

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Style** | Modern tech, upbeat but not distracting |
| **Tempo** | 110–130 BPM |
| **License** | Royalty-free (Artlist.io / Epidemic Sound) |
| **Consistency** | Same musical identity across all product demos |
