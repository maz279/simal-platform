## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Simal Partner Portal supports two-factor authentication for enhanced security:

| 2FA Method | Setup |
|---|---|
| **Authenticator App** (Recommended) | Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy — scan QR code during setup |
| **SMS** | Receive a 6-digit code via SMS to your registered phone number |
| **Email** | Receive a 6-digit code via email (less secure — app or SMS recommended) |

> **?? Security Recommendation:** All Premier and Elite Partner accounts require 2FA. Authorized Resellers are strongly encouraged to enable it.

## New Partner? Register for Portal Access

If your company has been approved as a Simal partner but you don't yet have portal access:

1. Click **[Register for Partner Portal Access](#)**
2. Enter your **Partner ID** (provided in your welcome email) and **work email**
3. Verify your email via the link we send
4. Set your password and configure 2FA
5. Log in and start using the portal

> ?? Portal access is granted only to employees of authorized Simal partners. Registration requests are verified against our partner database.
