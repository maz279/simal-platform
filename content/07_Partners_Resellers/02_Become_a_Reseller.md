## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I apply if I'm a startup without audited financials?**
Yes. Startups can apply for Authorized Reseller tier with a lower minimum commitment and shorter credit terms. We evaluate based on the founder/management team's industry experience and business plan.

**Q: What if my territory already has Simal partners?**
We welcome multiple partners in a territory — competition benefits everyone. However, Elite Partners may negotiate territory exclusivity for certain brands or customer segments.

**Q: Is my application information kept confidential?**
Absolutely. All application data is used solely for partnership evaluation and is never shared with third parties without your consent. Our team signs NDAs as part of their employment.

**Q: What happens if my application is declined?**
We'll provide specific reasons and, where possible, a path to re-apply. Common reasons: incomplete documentation, insufficient IT sales experience, or credit concerns. You may re-apply after 6 months.
