## 8. Data Subject Rights

Under UAE PDPL and GDPR (where applicable), you have the following rights:

### 8.1 Right to Access

You have the right to request confirmation of whether we process your personal data, and if so, access to that data along with information about the processing.

### 8.2 Right to Rectification

You have the right to have inaccurate personal data corrected and incomplete data completed.

### 8.3 Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data in certain circumstances, such as when:
- The data is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected
- You withdraw consent and no other legal basis exists
- The data has been unlawfully processed

### 8.4 Right to Restrict Processing

You have the right to restrict processing where:
- You contest the accuracy of the data
- The processing is unlawful but you oppose deletion
- We no longer need the data but you need it for legal claims

### 8.5 Right to Data Portability

You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format and have it transmitted to another controller where technically feasible.

### 8.6 Right to Object

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests, including profiling. You have an absolute right to object to direct marketing.

### 8.7 Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making

We do not currently engage in automated decision-making (including profiling) that produces legal effects or similarly significant effects on individuals. Should this change, we will update this policy and provide relevant notices.

### 8.8 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at:
- **Email:** info@simalme.com (Subject: "Data Subject Request")
- **Post:** Data Protection, Simal Technologies Middle East LLC, Office No: 201, Dar Al Riffa Building, Khalid Bin Al Waleed Road, Bur Dubai, PO Box: 49740, Dubai, UAE

We will respond within **30 calendar days** (or within the timeframe required by applicable law). We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

### 8.9 Right to Complain

If you believe your data protection rights have been violated, you have the right to lodge a complaint with:

- **UAE Data Office** (the competent authority under UAE PDPL)
- **Your local data protection authority** (if you are in the EEA)
- **Simal Technologies directly** — we encourage you to contact us first so we can address your concerns
