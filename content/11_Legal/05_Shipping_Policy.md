## 6. Customs, Duties & Taxes

### 6.1 International Customers

For shipments outside the UAE:
- The customer (consignee) is responsible for all import duties, customs fees, taxes, and clearance charges in the destination country
- Simal Technologies is not responsible for delays caused by customs clearance processes
- Some countries may require additional documentation (certificates, permits) — we will provide available documentation upon request

### 6.2 VAT (UAE)

All UAE domestic orders are subject to 5% VAT. VAT-compliant tax invoices are provided.

### 6.3 GCC VAT

- **Saudi Arabia:** 15% VAT
- **Bahrain:** 10% VAT
- **Oman:** 5% VAT

VAT is applied based on the destination country's regulations. VAT-compliant documentation is provided.

## 7. Delivery Requirements

### 7.1 Address Accuracy

- Customers are responsible for providing complete and accurate shipping addresses
- Include building name/number, street, area, city, and postal code (where applicable)
- Include a contact phone number with country code — required by most carriers
- Simal Technologies is not responsible for delays or non-delivery due to incorrect address information

### 7.2 Receiving the Shipment

- Someone must be available at the delivery address to receive and sign for the shipment
- For B2B deliveries, shipments may be received by authorized company representatives
- Inspect packages upon delivery for external damage before signing
- If external damage is visible, note it on the delivery receipt and contact us within 48 hours

### 7.3 Failed Delivery Attempts

- If delivery fails due to recipient unavailability or incorrect address, the carrier will attempt redelivery or hold the package at their local facility
- Additional delivery charges may apply for redelivery attempts
- Packages not collected from carrier facilities within the holding period may be returned to Simal at the customer's expense

## 10. Damaged or Lost Shipments

### 10.1 Damaged in Transit

- Inspect packages upon delivery. If external damage is visible, note it on the delivery receipt and take photos
- Report damaged shipments within 48 hours of delivery
- Submit a [Support Request](/contact/support) with photos of the damaged package and product

### 10.2 Lost Shipments

- If your tracking information shows no movement for an extended period, contact us for investigation
- We will work with the carrier to locate your shipment or process a replacement/refund

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I track my order?**
A: You will receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking information once your order is dispatched. B2B portal users can also track orders in their account dashboard.

**Q: Do you ship to my country?**
A: We ship to UAE, GCC, select African countries, and select CIS countries. Contact us at info@simalme.com to confirm availability for your specific location.

**Q: Who pays customs duties?**
A: The customer (consignee) is responsible for all import duties, customs fees, and taxes in the destination country.

**Q: Can I change my shipping address after ordering?**
A: Contact us immediately. If the order hasn't been dispatched, we can update the address. Once dispatched, changes may not be possible or may incur additional charges.

**Q: What if I need expedited shipping?**
A: Expedited shipping options are available for most destinations. Request this during your sales inquiry or contact your account manager.
