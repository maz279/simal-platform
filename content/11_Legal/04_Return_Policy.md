## 1. Overview

This Return Policy applies to IT hardware products purchased directly from Simal Technologies Middle East LLC. We are committed to ensuring our customers receive genuine, high-quality products. If you encounter an issue with your purchase, this policy outlines your options and the return process.

This policy should be read in conjunction with our [Refund Policy](/legal/refund-policy) and product-specific manufacturer warranty terms.

## 2. Return Eligibility

### 2.1 General Return Conditions

To be eligible for a return, products must meet ALL of the following conditions:

| Condition | Requirement |
|-----------|------------|
| **Timeframe** | Return request initiated within 14 calendar days of delivery/pickup |
| **Product Condition** | Product must be in original, unopened, and undamaged condition |
| **Packaging** | All original packaging, manuals, accessories, cables, and inserts must be intact and included |
| **Seals/Labels** | Manufacturer security seals and labels must be unbroken |
| **Proof of Purchase** | Original Simal Technologies invoice/receipt must be provided |
| **Serial Number Match** | Product serial number must match the serial number on the invoice |
| **RMA Authorization** | A Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number must be obtained before returning any product |

### 2.2 Eligible Return Reasons

| Reason | Eligible |
|--------|---------|
| **Dead on Arrival (DOA)** | Yes — within 7 calendar days of delivery |
| **Wrong Product Shipped** | Yes — within 7 calendar days of delivery |
| **Product Damaged in Transit** | Yes — must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photo evidence |
| **Manufacturing Defect** | Yes — subject to manufacturer warranty terms |
| **Change of Mind (Unopened)** | Partial — restocking fee may apply (see Section 2.4) |
| **Performance Not as Expected** | Case-by-case — subject to manufacturer assessment |

---

### 2.3 Non-Returnable Items

The following items CANNOT be returned:

| Item Category | Reason |
|--------------|--------|
| **Opened Software / Licenses** | License keys, activation codes — non-returnable once activated or revealed |
| **Opened Consumables** | Thermal paste, cleaning products, opened accessories |
| **Custom-Configured Products** | Products assembled or configured to customer specifications |
| **Special Order Items** | Products specifically ordered for a customer that are not part of regular inventory |
| **Products with Physical Damage** | Dropped, liquid-damaged, cracked, bent pins, burn marks |
| **Products with Missing Serial Numbers** | Tampered, removed, or unreadable serial numbers |
| **Products Purchased More Than 14 Days Ago** | Outside the return window (unless warranty claim) |
| **Products Purchased from Third-Party Resellers** | Must be returned to the reseller where purchased |
| **Clearance / "As-Is" Items** | Products sold as clearance, open-box, or "as-is" condition |

### 2.4 Restocking Fee

| Return Reason | Restocking Fee |
|--------------|---------------|
| DOA / Wrong Item / Damaged in Transit | No fee |
| Manufacturing Defect (confirmed) | No fee |
| Change of Mind (product unopened) | 15% of invoice value (minimum AED 50) |
| Change of Mind (product opened) | Not eligible for return |

## 3. Return Process (RMA)

### Step-by-Step Process

| Step | Action | Timeline |
|------|--------|---------|
| **Step 1: Submit Request** | Complete the [Support Request Form](/contact/support) or email info@simalme.com with your invoice number, product details, and reason for return. Include photos if the product is damaged or defective. | You initiate |
| **Step 2: RMA Issuance** | Our support team reviews your request. If eligible, an RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization) number is issued via email along with return instructions. If ineligible, we explain the reason. | Within 2 business days |
| **Step 3: Prepare Return** | Pack the product securely in its original packaging with all accessories. Write the RMA number clearly on the outside of the package. Include a copy of the original invoice and the RMA authorization email. | Per your timeline |
| **Step 4: Return to Warehouse** | Return the package to our JAFZA warehouse (see address below). You may use your preferred courier or arrange pickup through our team (charges may apply). | Within 7 days of RMA issuance |
| **Step 5: Inspection** | Our team inspects the returned product against RMA criteria: physical condition, serial number verification, completeness check, functional testing (if applicable). | 2-5 business days after receipt |
| **Step 6: Resolution** | Based on inspection: • Approved ? Replacement or refund processed • Rejected ? Product returned to customer or disposed (per customer instruction) | See Refund Policy |

## 5. B2B-Specific Return Terms

For business customers (resellers, system integrators, VARs), the following additional terms apply:

| Aspect | B2B Terms |
|--------|----------|
| **Return Window** | 14 calendar days from delivery (standard) or as specified in your trade agreement |
| **Bulk Orders (10+ units)** | Returns may be subject to additional inspection and batch testing |
| **Defect Rate** | If more than 5% of units in a bulk order are defective, contact your account manager for batch-level resolution |
| **Credit Account Customers** | Refunds processed as credit note against outstanding balance or future orders, unless otherwise agreed |
| **Custom Packaging/Labeling** | Products with custom B2B packaging cannot be returned unless defective |

## 6. Manufacturer Warranty Returns

For products that develop a fault after the 14-day return window but within the manufacturer's warranty period:

1. Check the warranty period for your product (see warranty information on your invoice or product packaging)
2. Submit a [Support Request](/contact/support) describing the issue in detail
3. Our team will assess whether the issue is covered under the manufacturer's warranty
4. If covered: RMA issued for warranty service (repair or replacement per manufacturer policy)
5. If not covered (physical damage, misuse, out of warranty): Options for paid repair or replacement provided

**Warranty periods vary by manufacturer and product category.** Refer to your product documentation or invoice for specific warranty terms.

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I return a product I opened and used?**
A: Generally, no — unless the product is defective. Opened and used products that are not defective cannot be returned under our change-of-mind policy.

**Q: How long does the inspection process take?**
A: Inspections are completed within 2-5 business days after we receive the returned product at our JAFZA warehouse.

**Q: Do I need the original packaging to return a product?**
A: Yes. All original packaging, manuals, cables, and accessories must be returned. Missing items may result in a reduced refund or return rejection.

**Q: What if I received the wrong product?**
A: Report it within 7 calendar days. We will arrange for the correct product to be shipped and for the wrong product to be collected at our expense.

**Q: Can I return products purchased from a Simal reseller?**
A: No. Products purchased from authorized Simal resellers must be returned to the reseller where you made the purchase.
