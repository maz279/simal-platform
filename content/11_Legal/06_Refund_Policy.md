## 1. Overview

This Refund Policy outlines how Simal Technologies Middle East LLC processes refunds for returned products, canceled orders, and other eligible refund scenarios. This policy should be read in conjunction with our [Return Policy](/legal/return-policy).

---

## 2. Refund Eligibility

### 2.1 When Refunds Are Issued

| Scenario | Refund Eligibility |
|----------|-------------------|
| **Return Approved (DOA / Defect / Wrong Item)** | Full refund of product price + original shipping (if paid) |
| **Return Approved (Change of Mind)** | Refund of product price minus 15% restocking fee. Original shipping costs are non-refundable. |
| **Order Canceled Before Dispatch** | Full refund of paid amount (no restocking fee) |
| **Product Out of Stock (after order)** | Full refund of paid amount |
| **Duplicate Payment** | Full refund of duplicate amount |
| **Price Difference / Billing Error** | Refund of the difference |

### 2.2 Non-Refundable Items

| Item | Policy |
|------|--------|
| **Shipping Charges** | Non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (wrong item, DOA, damaged) |
| **Customs / Import Duties** | Non-refundable by Simal; customer may apply to local customs authority for duty drawback |
| **Restocking Fee** | 15% of invoice value (change-of-mind returns); deducted from refund |
| **Bank Transfer / Processing Fees** | Non-refundable — actual bank charges incurred are deducted from the refund |
| **Software Licenses (activated)** | Non-refundable once license key is activated or revealed |
| **Special Order / Custom Products** | Non-refundable unless defective |

## 3. Refund Methods

Refunds are processed using the same method as the original payment, where possible:

| Original Payment Method | Refund Method |
|------------------------|-------------|
| **Bank Transfer / Telegraphic Transfer (TT)** | Bank transfer to the originating account |
| **Credit Card** | Refund to the same credit card (may take 5-15 business days to appear on statement) |
| **Cash (in-person)** | Bank transfer (refunds are not issued in cash) |
| **Credit Note (B2B Account)** | Credit applied to account balance against future orders |
| **Cheque** | Bank transfer |

### 3.1 B2B Credit Account Customers

For B2B customers with established credit accounts, refunds are typically processed as:
- **Credit Note:** Applied against your outstanding balance or future orders (faster processing)
- **Bank Transfer:** If specifically requested (may take longer)

## 4. Refund Timeline

| Stage | Timeline |
|-------|---------|
| **Return Received & Inspection Completed** | 2-5 business days after product arrives at JAFZA warehouse |
| **Refund Approved** | Within 2 business days after inspection is complete |
| **Refund Processed (Bank Transfer)** | 3-7 business days after approval (depends on bank processing) |
| **Refund Processed (Credit Card)** | 5-15 business days after approval (depends on card issuer) |
| **Credit Note Issued (B2B)** | Within 2 business days after inspection approval |

**Total estimated timeline from return receipt to refund receipt in your account: 7-15 business days** (for bank transfers) or **10-20 business days** (for credit card refunds).

---

## 5. Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued in the following situations:

| Situation | Partial Refund Amount |
|-----------|----------------------|
| **Missing Accessories / Packaging** | Refund reduced by the retail value of missing items |
| **Product Opened (change-of-mind approved as exception)** | Refund reduced by up to 30% based on product condition |
| **Bundle Purchase — Partial Return** | Refund for the returned item only, at the bundled price (not full individual retail price) |
| **Minor Cosmetic Damage (not affecting function)** | Refund reduced by 10-20% depending on damage assessment |

Partial refunds are communicated to the customer before processing. The customer has the option to have the product returned instead of accepting a partial refund (return shipping at customer's expense).

## 6. How to Request a Refund

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Submit a Return:** Follow the process in our [Return Policy](/legal/return-policy) to obtain an RMA number and return the product.
2. **Inspection:** Our team inspects the returned product. You will be notified of the results.
3. **Refund Approval:** If the return is approved for a refund, you will receive a refund confirmation email with the amount, method, and expected timeline.
4. **Refund Processing:** The refund is processed through the appropriate channel. Processing timelines vary by payment method (see Section 4).
5. **Confirmation:** You will receive a final confirmation once the refund has been issued.

## 7. Order Cancellation

### 7.1 Before Dispatch

- Orders can be canceled before they are dispatched from our warehouse
- Contact your sales representative or email info@simalme.com immediately
- Full refund issued with no restocking fee

### 7.2 After Dispatch

- Once an order is dispatched, it cannot be canceled
- You may refuse delivery, after which the product will be returned to our warehouse
- Standard return process and restocking fee (15%) will apply

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does it take to get my refund?**
A: From the date we receive and inspect your returned product, refunds typically take 7-15 business days to appear in your account for bank transfers, and 10-20 business days for credit card refunds.

**Q: Can I get a cash refund?**
A: No. All refunds are processed electronically — via bank transfer or back to the original credit card. We do not issue cash refunds.

**Q: The refund amount is less than I expected. Why?**
A: Deductions may include the 15% restocking fee (change-of-mind returns), original shipping costs (if non-refundable), missing accessories or packaging, or bank transfer fees. You will receive a breakdown with your refund confirmation.

**Q: I paid by credit card but the card has expired. How will I get my refund?**
A: Contact us with your updated banking details. Refunds are sent via bank transfer for expired cards.

**Q: Can I exchange a product instead of getting a refund?**
A: Yes. If you prefer a replacement product (same model) instead of a refund, specify this when submitting your return request. Exchanges are subject to product availability.

**Q: Who pays for return shipping on a refund?**
A: See our [Return Policy](/legal/return-policy) for details. Generally, we cover return shipping for DOA, wrong items, and damaged goods. Change-of-mind returns are at the customer's expense.
