## Quote Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| **Pending** | Quote request submitted — awaiting Simal sales team response |
| **Sent** | Quote generated and sent — awaiting customer review |
| **Viewed** | Quote opened/viewed by customer |
| **Accepted** | Quote accepted by customer — ready for order conversion |
| **Declined** | Quote declined by customer |
| **Expired** | Quote validity period has passed without action |
| **Converted to Order** | Quote has been converted into a confirmed order |

## Accepting a Quote

When a customer clicks **Accept Quote**:

1. Confirmation modal: "Are you sure you want to accept Quote #{Quote #} for AED {Total}?"
2. Customer clicks "Confirm Acceptance"
3. Quote status updated to "Accepted"
4. Simal sales team notified
5. Customer can now convert the accepted quote to an order

---

## Converting Quote to Order

When a customer clicks **Convert to Order**:

1. Navigate to conversion page showing quote items
2. Customer confirms quantities and shipping address
3. Customer selects payment method or agrees to terms
4. Order created with reference to quote
5. Quote status updated to "Converted to Order"
6. Order appears in [Order Management](/b2b/orders)

---

## Declining a Quote

When a customer clicks **Decline Quote**:

1. Decline reason (optional) — dropdown: Price too high • No longer needed • Purchased elsewhere • Product specifications don't match • Other
2. Comment field (optional)
3. Confirm decline
4. Quote status updated to "Declined"
5. Simal sales team notified — may follow up

---

## Quote Expiry

- Standard quote validity: **14 calendar days** from quote date
- Expiring quotes show a countdown timer in red (last 3 days)
- Expired quotes: status changes to "Expired" — cannot be accepted or converted

### Renewing an Expired Quote

- Click **Request Renewal** on an expired quote
- Sales team notified and may issue a new quote with updated pricing
- New quote generated with new quote number, date, and validity period
