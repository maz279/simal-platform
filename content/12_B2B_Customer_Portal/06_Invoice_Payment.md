## Invoice Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| **Unpaid** | Invoice issued — payment not yet received |
| **Paid** | Payment received and confirmed |
| **Overdue** | Payment not received by the due date |
| **Partially Paid** | Partial payment received; balance outstanding |
| **Credit Note** | Credit issued against the account |

## Invoice Detail View

| Section | Content |
|---------|--------|
| **Invoice Header** | Invoice #, Date, Due Date, Customer PO #, Order Reference |
| **Bill To** | Company Name, Address, VAT Number |
| **Items** | Brand, Model, Description, Qty, Unit Price, Line Total |
| **Totals** | Subtotal, VAT (5%), Shipping, Grand Total |
| **Payment Instructions** | Bank name, Account name, IBAN, SWIFT, Reference (Invoice #) |
| **Actions** | Download PDF • Pay Now (if unpaid) |

### After Making Payment

1. Send proof of payment to info@simalme.com with subject: "Payment — Invoice #{Invoice #}"
2. Our finance team will verify the payment (typically 1-2 business days)
3. Invoice status updates to "Paid" once confirmed
4. You can track payment status in this portal
