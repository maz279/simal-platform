## Settings Sections

### 1. Company Profile

| # | Field | Type | Description |
|---|-------|------|------------|
| 1 | **Company Name** | Text (read-only) | Legal company name — to change, contact support |
| 2 | **Trade License Number** | Text (read-only) | To update, upload new trade license |
| 3 | **VAT / TAX Number** | Text | Update your VAT registration number |
| 4 | **Company Website** | URL | Your company's website |
| 5 | **Company Type** | Dropdown (read-only) | To change, contact support |
| 6 | **Update Trade License** | File Upload | Upload renewed trade license (PDF/JPG, max 5MB) |
| 7 | **Save Changes** | Button | Save profile updates |

### 3. Password & Security

| # | Field | Type | Description |
|---|-------|------|------------|
| 1 | **Current Password** | Password | Enter current password to make changes |
| 2 | **New Password** | Password | Min 8 characters, uppercase, lowercase, number, special character |
| 3 | **Confirm New Password** | Password | Re-enter new password |
| 4 | **Change Password** | Button | Update password |

#### Password Requirements

- Minimum 8 characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one lowercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character (!@#$%^&*)
- Cannot be the same as your previous 3 passwords

---

### 4. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

| Setting | Description |
|---------|------------|
| **Current Status** | {Enabled / Disabled} |
| **Enable 2FA** | Set up authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, Microsoft Authenticator). Scan QR code ? Verify with code ? Save backup recovery codes. |
| **Disable 2FA** | Turn off 2FA (requires current password + 2FA code) |
| **Recovery Codes** | View or regenerate backup recovery codes (10 single-use codes) |

> **Strongly Recommended:** Enable 2FA to protect your account and company data.

### 7. Account Deactivation

> **?? Warning:** Deactivating your account will:
> - Disable access to the B2B Portal
> - Cancel any pending quotes
> - Not affect existing orders (they will still be processed)
> - Retain your data per our Privacy Policy

**To deactivate:**
1. Click "Request Account Deactivation"
2. Provide reason (optional)
3. Confirm with password
4. Account deactivated — can be reactivated by contacting support
