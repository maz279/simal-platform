## Key Features

### Next-Gen DDR5 Architecture
DDR5 doubles the bandwidth of DDR4 while consuming less power (1.1V vs 1.2V). On-module voltage regulation delivers cleaner power for improved stability.

### Professional Reliability
Built and tested to Crucial's stringent professional standards — 24/7 reliability for workstations that can't afford downtime.

### Plug-and-Play Performance
Standard JEDEC profiles ensure compatibility out of the box. XMP 3.0 and AMD EXPO profiles available for one-click overclocking on supported platforms.

### Aluminum Heat Spreader
Efficient thermal dissipation keeps modules cool under sustained professional workloads — no throttling, no instability.

### Dual 32-bit Subchannels Per DIMM
DDR5's new architecture improves memory access efficiency, delivering smoother multitasking with fewer stalls.
