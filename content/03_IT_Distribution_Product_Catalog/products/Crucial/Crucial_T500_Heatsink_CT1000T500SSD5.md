## PS5 Installation

1. Power off PS5 and remove the side panel
2. Locate the M.2 expansion slot
3. Remove the screw and spacer
4. Insert the T500 Heatsink into the M.2 slot
5. Secure with the screw and spacer
6. Replace the side panel
7. Power on — PS5 will format and benchmark the drive automatically
