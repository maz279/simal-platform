## DDR4 vs DDR5 — Which Do You Need?

| Feature | **Crucial DDR4 Pro** | Crucial DDR5 |
|---------|--------------------|-------------|
| Platform | Older DDR4 systems | Latest-gen Intel/AMD |
| Speed | Up to 3,200 MT/s | Up to 5,600+ MT/s |
| Voltage | 1.2V | 1.1V |
| Best For | Upgrading existing DDR4 PCs | New builds on latest platforms |

> ⚠️ **Important:** DDR4 and DDR5 are not interchangeable. Verify your motherboard supports DDR4 before purchasing.
