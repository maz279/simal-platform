## Key Features

### Unprecedented Speed
12,400 MB/s sequential read — transfer a 100GB file in under 9 seconds. The T700 rewrites expectations for what storage performance means.

### PCIe Gen5 × NVMe 2.0
Leverages the full bandwidth of PCIe Gen5 with the latest NVMe 2.0 protocol for maximum efficiency and throughput.

### 1.5M IOPS
Three times the random read IOPS of Gen4 drives — eliminate micro-stutter in games, lag in professional applications, and wait time in everything.

### Integrated Premium Heatsink
Factory-installed heatsink with active cooling design maintains peak performance even under sustained Gen5 loads. Required for Gen5 operation.

### Microsoft DirectStorage Optimized
Fully compliant with DirectStorage 1.1 for GPU-decompressed asset streaming — the next generation of gaming is built for drives like this.
