## Key Features

### NVMe Performance, SATA Price
Experience up to 6× faster load times than traditional SATA SSDs — boot Windows in seconds, launch applications instantly, and load games faster.

### Massive Capacities
Available in up to **4TB**, giving you room for your entire game library, media collection, or creative projects on a single drive.

### Advanced QLC NAND
Micron's 176-layer QLC NAND provides an excellent balance of performance, capacity, and affordability — making NVMe accessible to everyone.

### Dynamic Write Acceleration
Intelligent SLC caching dynamically adjusts to provide burst write performance when you need it most.

Optimized power efficiency extends battery life in laptops, consuming less power than both traditional hard drives and previous-gen SSDs.
