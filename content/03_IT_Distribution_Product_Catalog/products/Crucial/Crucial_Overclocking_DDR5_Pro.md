## Key Features

### Extreme Overclocking Headroom
Hand-selected ICs and enhanced PCB design provide the headroom needed for aggressive frequency and timing tuning. XMP 3.0 and EXPO profiles enable one-click overclocking for instant performance gains.

### Premium Thermal Design
An aggressive aluminum heat spreader with optimized fin geometry keeps modules cool even at elevated voltages — critical for maintaining stability during extended gaming sessions and benchmark runs.

### Dual Overclocking Profiles
XMP 3.0 supports up to 5 customizable profiles. AMD EXPO certification ensures optimized performance on Ryzen platforms with a single BIOS toggle.

### Gaming-Optimized Timings
Tuned for low latency at high frequencies — the combination competitive gamers need for maximum frame rates and minimum input lag.

Extensively validated on both Intel 600/700-series and AMD AM5 platforms for broad compatibility.
