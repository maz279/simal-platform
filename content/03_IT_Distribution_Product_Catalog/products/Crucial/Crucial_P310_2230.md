## Power Up Your Handheld

The **Crucial P310 2230** brings Gen4 NVMe performance to the ultra-compact M.2 2230 form factor. Designed for handheld gaming devices like the **Steam Deck** and **ASUS ROG Ally**, as well as Microsoft Surface tablets and ultra-thin notebooks, the P310 2230 delivers dramatic storage and speed upgrades where standard 2280 SSDs simply don't fit.

## Installation Note

The M.2 2230 form factor is significantly shorter than the standard 2280. Always verify your device's SSD slot form factor before purchasing. This drive is **only compatible with M.2 2230 slots**.
