## Key Features

### Blazing Gen4 Performance
Sequential reads up to 7,400 MB/s and writes up to 7,000 MB/s — load AAA games in seconds, transfer large media files almost instantly, and eliminate productivity bottlenecks.

### Microsoft DirectStorage Optimized
The T500 is fully optimized for Microsoft DirectStorage, enabling GPU-decompressed asset streaming for next-generation gaming experiences with near-instant level loads.

### Advanced NAND Technology
Built on Micron's 232-layer TLC NAND — the industry's most advanced flash memory — for exceptional performance density and reliability.

### Power Efficient
Despite its flagship performance, the T500 consumes up to 40% less power per watt than previous-gen Crucial SSDs, extending battery life in laptops.

### Heatsink Option Available
For sustained write-intensive workloads, the T500 is also available with an integrated heatsink (CT1000T500SSD5) to maintain peak performance under thermal load.
