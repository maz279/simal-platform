## Key Features

### Laptop-Optimized Performance
DDR5 SODIMM modules deliver up to 5,600 MT/s in the power-efficient, compact form factor that laptops demand.

### Lower Power, Longer Battery
At just 1.1V, DDR5 SODIMMs consume less power than DDR4 — helping extend your laptop's battery life while delivering more performance.

### Easy Upgrade
Most laptops with accessible memory slots can be upgraded in under 10 minutes. Check your laptop's service manual for compatibility and installation instructions.

### On-Die ECC
Internal error correction improves data integrity without the overhead of full ECC — ideal for professionals who rely on their laptop for critical work.

### Compact Form Factor
The SODIMM form factor is approximately half the length of a standard desktop DIMM, designed specifically for the space constraints of portable devices.

## Before You Upgrade

1. **Check compatibility** — Use Crucial's System Scanner or consult your laptop manual
2. **Verify accessibility** — Some laptops require partial disassembly to access memory slots
3. **Match existing specs** — For dual-channel performance, use matched pairs of identical modules
