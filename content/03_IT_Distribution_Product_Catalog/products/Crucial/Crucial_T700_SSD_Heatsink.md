### Record-Breaking Speed
Industry-leading 12,400 MB/s sequential reads — faster than any PCIe Gen4 drive can achieve.

### Active-Cooling Heatsink
The premium heatsink includes an integrated micro-fan and optimized fin stack to dissipate the heat generated at Gen5 speeds — ensuring zero throttling, zero compromise.

Store your entire game library, 8K video projects, and virtual machines all on a single drive. Available in 1TB, 2TB, and 4TB configurations.
