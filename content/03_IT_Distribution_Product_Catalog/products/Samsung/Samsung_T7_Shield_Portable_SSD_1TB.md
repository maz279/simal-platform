## Key Features

### IP65 Dust & Water Resistant
Certified protection against dust ingress and low-pressure water jets — works reliably in rain, dusty construction sites, sandy beaches, and industrial environments where standard SSDs would fail.

### 3-Meter Drop Protection
Withstands drops from up to 3 meters (nearly 10 feet) — purpose-built for field professionals who work on ladders, scaffolding, rigs, and outdoor locations.

### 1,050 MB/s Sequential Read
USB 3.2 Gen 2 speeds rival internal SATA SSDs — edit 4K video directly from the drive, transfer projects in seconds, and boot virtual machines on the go.

### AES 256-Bit Hardware Encryption
Military-grade encryption protects sensitive corporate data, client files, and personal information — optional password protection via Samsung Magician software.

### Dynamic Thermal Guard
Built-in heat management prevents overheating during sustained transfers — consistent performance even under heavy workloads.

### Ultra-Compact Form Factor
At just 98 grams and 13mm thin, the T7 Shield is smaller than most wallets — comfortably fits in any gear bag, pocket, or pelican case.

### Dual Cable Included
USB-C to USB-C and USB-C to USB-A cables included — connect to latest laptops, legacy desktops, gaming consoles, and mobile devices without adapters.
