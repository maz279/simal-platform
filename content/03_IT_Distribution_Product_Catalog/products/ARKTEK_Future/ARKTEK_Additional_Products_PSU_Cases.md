## Coming Soon — ARKTEK Power Supplies

ARKTEK power supplies are engineered for reliability and efficiency, covering the full spectrum from entry-level office builds to high-wattage gaming and workstation configurations.

### Anticipated PSU Range

| Tier | Wattage Range | Target Use Case |
|------|--------------|----------------|
| **Entry-Level** | 400W – 500W | Office PCs, basic desktops |
| **Mid-Range** | 550W – 650W | Mainstream gaming, content creation |
| **High-Performance** | 750W – 850W | High-end gaming, workstations |
| **Enthusiast** | 1000W+ | Multi-GPU, extreme overclocking |

### Expected Features

- **80 PLUS Certification** — Bronze, Gold, and Platinum efficiency ratings
- **Active PFC** — Power factor correction for stable, efficient power delivery
- **Silent Operation** — Temperature-controlled 120mm/140mm fans with zero-RPM mode on select models
- **Full Modular Cabling** — On mid-range and above for clean builds
- **Japanese Capacitors** — Premium components for long-term reliability
- **Multiple Protections** — OVP, UVP, OPP, OCP, SCP, OTP

## Coming Soon — ARKTEK PC Cases

ARKTEK PC cases combine functional design with modern aesthetics, from clean office-oriented chassis to gaming cases with tempered glass panels and addressable RGB lighting.

### Anticipated Case Range

| Tier | Form Factor | Key Features |
|------|------------|-------------|
| **Budget-Friendly** | Mid-Tower | Clean design, good airflow, value-focused |
| **Mid-Range Gaming** | Mid-Tower / ATX | Tempered glass side panel, RGB fans, cable management |
| **High-End** | Full-Tower | Premium materials, extensive radiator support, advanced airflow |

### Expected Features

- **Tempered Glass Panels** — Showcase your build with edge-to-edge glass
- **Addressable RGB** — Integrated ARGB fans and controllers
- **Optimized Airflow** — Mesh front panels and filtered intakes
- **Cable Management** — Generous routing space with rubber grommets
- **Tool-Less Design** — Drive bays and panels for easy installation
- **Radiator Support** — 240mm, 280mm, and 360mm AIO compatibility
