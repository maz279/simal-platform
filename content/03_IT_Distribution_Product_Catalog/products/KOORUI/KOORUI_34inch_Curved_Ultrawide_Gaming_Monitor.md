## Key Features

### 34" 1000R Curved Immersion
The 1000R curvature matches the natural curve of the human eye — every point on screen is equidistant from your eyes, reducing distortion and eye strain while maximizing peripheral immersion.

### WQHD 3440×1440 Resolution
Nearly 2.4× the pixels of Full HD — sharper details, wider field of view, and more screen real estate for multitasking. See enemy movements in your peripheral vision before they see you.

### 165Hz + 1ms Response
Ultra-fluid motion at 165 frames per second with 1ms MPRT — eliminates ghosting in fast-paced action and competitive esports titles.

### 4000:1 Contrast Ratio
VA panel technology delivers deep, inky blacks and bright highlights — 4× the contrast of standard IPS panels for cinematic depth in horror, RPG, and atmospheric games.

### PIP & PBP Multitasking
Picture-in-Picture and Picture-by-Picture modes let you display two input sources simultaneously — game on one side, Discord/strategy guide on the other.

### Adaptive Sync (FreeSync Premium + G-Sync)
Premium variable refresh rate technology ensures tear-free, stutter-free gameplay across AMD and NVIDIA GPUs with LFC (Low Framerate Compensation).

### Ergonomic Stand
Height-adjustable stand with tilt functionality for optimal viewing comfort during marathon gaming sessions.
