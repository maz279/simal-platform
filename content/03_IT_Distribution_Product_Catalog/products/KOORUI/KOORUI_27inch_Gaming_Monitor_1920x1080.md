## Key Features

### 200Hz Ultra-Smooth Gaming
At 200 frames per second, every movement is buttery smooth — from fast-twitch FPS aiming to competitive MOBA team fights. The 200Hz refresh rate renders over 3× more frames per second than standard 60Hz monitors.

### 1ms MPRT Response
Near-instant pixel response eliminates ghosting and motion blur — critical for tracking fast-moving targets in competitive shooters and racing games.

### Fast IPS Panel
Combines IPS color accuracy and wide viewing angles with gaming-grade speed — no compromise between visuals and performance.

### HDR400 with 90% DCI-P3
High Dynamic Range support and wide color gamut deliver richer shadows, brighter highlights, and cinema-quality color reproduction.

### Adaptive-Sync Technology
Dynamically matches the monitor's refresh rate to your GPU's frame output — eliminates screen tearing, stuttering, and input lag. Compatible with both AMD FreeSync and NVIDIA G-Sync.

### Triple Input Connectivity
2× HDMI 2.0 and 1× DisplayPort 1.4 offer flexible connectivity for gaming PCs, consoles (PS5, Xbox Series X|S), and streaming devices.

### Eye Comfort
Low Blue Light mode and flicker-free backlight technology reduce eye strain during extended gaming or work sessions.
