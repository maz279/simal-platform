## Key Features

### 1500R Curved Display
The gentle 1500R curvature enhances depth perception and draws you into the action — more immersive than flat panels without the extreme curve of 1000R designs. Perfect for solo gaming sessions.

### 180Hz Smooth Gaming
At 180 frames per second, enjoy fluid motion that makes fast-paced gameplay feel responsive and natural. Combined with 1ms MPRT, motion blur is virtually eliminated.

### VA Panel with 99% sRGB
Rich colors covering 99% of the sRGB spectrum with a 3000:1 contrast ratio — deeper blacks than IPS panels for atmospheric games, horror titles, and cinematic content.

### Adaptive-Sync Technology
Synchronizes the monitor's refresh rate with your GPU to eliminate screen tearing and stuttering — delivering smooth, artifact-free visuals in every game.

### Triple Input Connectivity
DisplayPort 1.2 and dual HDMI 2.0 ports — connect your gaming PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and streaming devices without swapping cables.

### Eye Care Certified
Flicker-free backlight technology and Low Blue Light mode reduce eye fatigue during long gaming sessions, late-night raids, or work-from-home days.

### VESA Mount Ready
100×100mm VESA pattern compatible with monitor arms and wall mounts — free up desk space and achieve the perfect viewing angle.
