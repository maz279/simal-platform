## Key Features

### IPS Panel Technology
In-Plane Switching delivers consistent, accurate colors from nearly any viewing angle (178°/178°) — unlike VA or TN panels that wash out when viewed off-center. Perfect for collaborative work where multiple people view the same screen.

### Full HD 1080p Resolution
Crisp 1920×1080 resolution renders text sharply and images cleanly — essential for document work, web browsing, and media consumption.

### 75Hz Refresh Rate
Smoother than standard 60Hz — noticeable improvement in cursor movement, scrolling, and video playback. Reduces perceived flicker for more comfortable all-day use.

### 5ms Response Time (Overdrive)
Quick pixel response minimizes ghosting in fast-moving content — perfectly adequate for casual gaming, video streaming, and productivity.

### Free-Sync Technology
Variable refresh rate support eliminates screen tearing in videos and casual games — a feature not commonly found in monitors at this price point.

### Frameless Ultra-Slim Design
Near-invisible bezels maximize the display area and enable seamless multi-monitor configurations for trading desks, control rooms, and productivity workstations.

### Dual Connectivity (HDMI + VGA)
Modern HDMI for PCs, laptops, and media players alongside legacy VGA for older systems and thin clients — maximum compatibility across mixed IT environments.
