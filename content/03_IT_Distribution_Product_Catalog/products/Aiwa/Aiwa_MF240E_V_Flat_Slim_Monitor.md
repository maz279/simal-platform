## Key Features

### 24-Inch Full HD Display
The ideal size for desktop productivity — comfortably fits two documents side-by-side, reduces the need for excessive scrolling, and provides an immersive experience for movies and games.

### VA Panel with Rich Contrast
Vertical Alignment technology delivers deeper blacks and punchier colors compared to TN panels — great for watching videos, viewing photos, and working in dimly lit environments.

### 75Hz Smooth Refresh
25% more frames per second than standard 60Hz — smoother cursor movement, less motion blur while scrolling, and a more fluid overall desktop experience.

### Free-Sync Technology
Eliminates screen tearing and stuttering during video playback and casual gaming — dynamically matches refresh rate to content frame rate.

### 5ms Response (Overdrive)
Quick enough for casual gaming and fast enough that video content looks crisp — no distracting ghosting in everyday use.

### Frameless Design
Ultra-thin bezels maximize screen area and create a modern aesthetic — ideal for reception desks, executive offices, and customer-facing installations.

### VESA Wall Mount Ready
Mount directly on walls or monitor arms — free up desk space completely and achieve the perfect ergonomic viewing position.

### 16.7 Million Colors
Full 8-bit color depth support renders gradients smoothly without banding — important for photo viewing, presentations, and design-adjacent work.
