## Key Features

### Full HD 1080p Clarity
1920×1080 resolution delivers sharp, detailed images — ideal for spreadsheets, documents, web browsing, and multimedia content.

### 75Hz Smooth Motion
A step above standard 60Hz monitors — 75Hz refresh rate provides visibly smoother cursor movement, scrolling, and video playback, reducing eye fatigue during extended use.

### A+ Grade VA Panel
Premium VA panel technology with 3000:1 contrast ratio — deeper blacks and richer colors than standard TN panels. A+ grade ensures minimal backlight bleed and dead pixels.

### Free-Sync Technology
Eliminates screen tearing during video playback and casual gaming — synchronizes the monitor's refresh rate with your GPU output.

### Frameless Ultra-Slim Design
Near-borderless display maximizes screen real estate and looks modern in any setting — perfect for dual-monitor setups where bezels would interrupt the view.

### Dual Input (HDMI + VGA)
Connect modern devices via HDMI and legacy systems via VGA — broad compatibility with PCs, laptops, media players, and CCTV DVRs.

### VESA Mount Compatible
Standard VESA mounting pattern for monitor arms and wall mounts — reclaim desk space and position the screen at the perfect ergonomic height.
