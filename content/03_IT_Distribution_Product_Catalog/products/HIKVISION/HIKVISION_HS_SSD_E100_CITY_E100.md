## Key Features

### 3D Stacking Technology
Vertical NAND cell stacking increases density, improves reliability, and reduces cost — making SSD performance accessible for every computer.

### Better Data Safety
Advanced error correction algorithms and power-loss protection mechanisms safeguard your data against corruption from unexpected power interruptions.

### High Performance
Up to **560 MB/s sequential read** — that's over 5× faster than a typical 7,200 RPM hard drive. Boot Windows in seconds instead of minutes.

### Universal Compatibility
The standard 2.5" SATA form factor fits virtually any desktop or laptop with a SATA port. A drop-in replacement for traditional hard drives — no new cables, no new drivers.

### Low Power, Silent Operation
No moving parts means zero noise, lower power consumption, and better shock resistance compared to mechanical hard drives.
