### USB 3.2 Gen 2 Speed
Transfer a 10GB file in approximately 10 seconds — up to 10× faster than traditional external hard drives.

### Ultra-Compact Design
Slim enough to slip into a pocket, bag, or laptop sleeve. Perfect for professionals who need fast storage wherever they work.

Works with Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, PlayStation, Xbox, and any device with a USB port. Pre-formatted exFAT for cross-platform use out of the box.

No external power required. No drivers to install. Just plug in and access your files instantly.
