## Key Features

### Blazing PCIe 4.0 Speed
Sequential reads up to 7,450 MB/s — saturating the PCIe 4.0 bus for near-instant boot times, rapid game loads, and smooth creative workflows.

### Advanced Thermal Dissipation
Engineered with enhanced thermal management to maintain consistent performance during sustained read/write operations — no throttling when you need speed most.

### 3D TLC NAND
High-quality Triple-Level Cell NAND flash provides an optimal balance of performance, endurance, and capacity.

### High Performance with New Technology
Leveraging the latest controller and NAND technologies, the FUTURE delivers exceptional random read/write IOPS for responsive multitasking.

### Broad Compatibility
Standard M.2 2280 form factor fits most modern desktop and laptop PCIe 4.0 slots. Backward compatible with PCIe 3.0 at reduced speeds.
