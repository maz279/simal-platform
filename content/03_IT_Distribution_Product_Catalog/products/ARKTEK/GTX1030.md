## Entry-Level Gaming, Elevated

The **GTX1030** is the ideal entry point into discrete graphics. With 2GB of GDDR5 memory on a 64-bit interface and triple display outputs, it delivers smooth esports gaming at 1080p, flawless HD media playback, and a dramatic upgrade from integrated graphics — all without requiring an external power connector.

## Key Features

### GDDR5 Memory
Faster GDDR5 memory (vs DDR3) provides noticeably better gaming and multimedia performance.

### Slot-Powered
No external power cables needed — installs in any PC with a PCIe x16 slot and a 300W PSU.

### Triple Display Output
HDMI, DVI, and VGA for flexible multi-monitor support.

### Compact Size
Fits in slim desktops and small form factor PCs — ideal for HTPCs and office upgrades.
