## Key Features

### Global Compatibility
Slide-out plugs for UK, EU, US, and AU socket types cover virtually every country in the world — no more carrying multiple adapters.

### All-in-One Solution
Combines a universal plug adapter with dual USB charging ports — charge your laptop (via AC passthrough), phone, and tablet simultaneously from one wall socket.

### Built-In Surge Protection
Protects connected devices from voltage fluctuations common in international travel — essential for sensitive electronics like laptops and smartphones.

### Dual USB Charging
Two USB-A ports charge smartphones, power banks, wireless earbuds, smartwatches, and other USB devices without needing separate chargers.

### Ultra-Compact & Portable
Pocket-sized design (fits in laptop bag side pocket) — the ultimate travel minimalist's power solution.

### Worldwide Voltage Support
100–240V AC input supports voltage standards globally — works in North America (110V), Europe/Asia/Middle East/Africa (220–240V).
