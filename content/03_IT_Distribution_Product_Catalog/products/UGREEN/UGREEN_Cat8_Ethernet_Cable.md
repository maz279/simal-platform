## Key Features

### 40 Gbps Speed
Cat8 delivers up to 40 Gbps — 4× the bandwidth of Cat6 and ideal for data centers, server rooms, and high-performance workstations.

### Pure Copper Conductors
100% pure copper ensures maximum conductivity and signal integrity — no copper-clad aluminum (CCA) compromises.

### Premium Braided Jacket
Durable nylon braiding protects against cuts, kinks, and daily wear while providing a premium look and feel.

### Professional Shielding
Each twisted pair is individually foil-shielded, with an additional overall braid — maximum protection against interference.
