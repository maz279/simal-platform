## Key Features

### 10 Gbps Ready
Cat7 supports up to 10 Gbps for future-proof networking — ideal for NAS systems, media servers, and high-bandwidth applications.

### Superior Shielding
S/FTP shielding rejects electromagnetic interference (EMI) and crosstalk — stable connections even in electrically noisy environments.

### Braided Modular Plugs
Reinforced connector housings withstand repeated plug/unplug cycles without damage.

### Flat or Round Design
Choose the flat variant for under-carpet routing or the round shielded version for maximum protection.
