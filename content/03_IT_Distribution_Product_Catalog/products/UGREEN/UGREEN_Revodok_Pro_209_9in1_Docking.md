## Key Features

### Quad Display Output
Two HDMI and two DisplayPort outputs — drive up to four external displays for the ultimate productivity setup (dual 4K@60Hz on Windows and macOS).

### Blazing 10Gbps Transfer
USB 3.1 Gen2 at 10Gbps — transfer a 20GB file in approximately 16 seconds. Twice as fast as standard 5Gbps hubs.

### 100W Power Delivery
Charge your laptop at full speed while using every port on the dock. One USB-C connection handles power, displays, data, and network.

### Gigabit Ethernet
Stable, low-latency wired networking — essential for video calls, large file transfers, and cloud workflows.

### Premium Aluminum Build
Sleek aluminum alloy housing dissipates heat efficiently while matching premium laptop aesthetics.
