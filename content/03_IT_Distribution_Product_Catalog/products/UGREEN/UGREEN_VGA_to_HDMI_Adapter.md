## Bridge Legacy to Modern

The **UGREEN VGA to HDMI Adapter** solves a common problem: connecting a device with only a VGA output (older laptop, desktop, or legacy equipment) to a modern HDMI display. Active signal conversion ensures compatibility where simple cables cannot work.

## Key Features

### Active Signal Conversion
Unlike passive VGA-to-HDMI cables, this adapter actively converts the analog VGA signal to digital HDMI — ensuring compatibility with all HDMI displays.

### USB Audio Support
Includes a USB connector that carries audio to the HDMI output — because VGA is video-only, this is essential for complete A/V conversion.

### Full HD Support
Converts VGA output to crisp 1080p HDMI for modern monitors and TVs.

### Plug & Play
No drivers or software required — connect and go.

---

## Important Note

⚠️ This adapter converts **VGA source → HDMI display**. It does NOT work in reverse (HDMI source → VGA display). For connecting a modern HDMI device to a VGA monitor, a different adapter is required.
