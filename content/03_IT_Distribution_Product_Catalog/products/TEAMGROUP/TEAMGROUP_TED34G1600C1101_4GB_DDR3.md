### 1600 MHz DDR3 Speed
Optimal performance for DDR3-era systems — faster than DDR3-1333 and compatible with most LGA1155, LGA1150, AM3+, and FM2+ platforms.

### Standard 1.5V Operation
Runs at JEDEC standard 1.5V for broad motherboard compatibility and stable operation — no overvolting required.

Standard desktop form factor compatible with most DDR3 desktop motherboards from major manufacturers including ASUS, Gigabyte, MSI, ASRock, Intel, and HP/Dell/Lenovo OEM boards.
