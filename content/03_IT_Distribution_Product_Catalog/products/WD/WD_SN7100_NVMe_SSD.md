## Key Features

### PCIe Gen4 ×4 Performance
With sequential reads up to 7,250 MB/s, the SN7100 loads games, boots Windows, and transfers large files in the blink of an eye — up to 2× faster than PCIe Gen3 SSDs.

### Best-in-Class Power Efficiency
Engineered for maximum performance-per-watt — the SN7100 excels in laptops where battery life and thermal constraints matter. Consistently ranked as one of the most power-efficient Gen4 SSDs on the market.

### DRAM-less with HMB
Uses Host Memory Buffer (HMB) technology to leverage system RAM for caching — achieves near-DRAM performance without the cost, power draw, or heat of a dedicated DRAM chip.

### WD_BLACK Gaming DNA
Built on WD's gaming SSD heritage — optimized for fast game loads, smooth open-world streaming, and rapid level transitions. DirectStorage ready for next-gen gaming.

### Compact M.2 2280 Form Factor
The industry-standard M.2 2280 size fits virtually all modern laptops, desktops, and even PlayStation 5 (with heatsink) — universal compatibility.

### 5-Year Warranty
Backed by WD's 5-year limited warranty and industry-leading endurance ratings — built for years of intensive use.
