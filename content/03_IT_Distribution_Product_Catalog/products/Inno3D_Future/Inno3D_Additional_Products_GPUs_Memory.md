## Inno3D Technologies — Expanding Our Portfolio

**Inno3D Technologies**, founded in 1998 in Hong Kong, is a leading manufacturer of high-performance graphics cards, memory modules, and PC components. As part of our brand expansion strategy, Simal Technologies is adding Inno3D to its authorized distribution portfolio, bringing Inno3D's complete range of NVIDIA and AMD graphics cards and memory modules to the Middle East, Africa, and CIS markets.
