## Key Features

### AMD AM4 Platform
Supports a massive range of AMD Ryzen processors — from 1st Gen Ryzen through Ryzen 5000 series. Build anything from a basic office PC to a powerful content creation workstation on one platform.

### DDR4 Memory Support
Dual DIMM slots support up to 64GB of DDR4 memory with overclocking speeds up to 4133 MHz (OC) — flexible memory configurations for any workload.

### M.2 PCIe Gen3 ×4
Dedicated M.2 slot supports NVMe SSDs at PCIe 3.0 ×4 speeds — install a fast boot drive for rapid system startup and application loading.

### Triple Display Outputs
Simultaneous HDMI, DVI-D, and VGA outputs support up to three monitors — perfect for productivity workstations, trading desks, and digital signage.

### Core Boost Technology
MSI's optimized power delivery with digital PWM controller ensures stable power to the CPU — reliable performance under sustained workloads.

### Micro-ATX Versatility
Compact mATX form factor fits in both standard ATX and compact mATX cases — flexible for office desktops, home theater PCs, and budget gaming rigs.

### Gigabit LAN
Reliable Realtek Gigabit Ethernet for stable, high-speed wired network connectivity — essential for business environments and online gaming.

### MSI Quality & Reliability
Built with MSI's Military Class components and extensive validation testing — engineered for long-term reliability in business and consumer environments.
