## Key Features

### 14" 2K (2560×1440) Display
With 1.8× the pixels of Full HD, the 2K display renders text razor-sharp and images with stunning detail. The IPS panel ensures wide viewing angles and accurate colors — ideal for design review, data analysis, and content creation.

### Ultraportable Design
At approximately 1.4 kg with a slim profile, it's built for professionals who work across offices, client sites, coffee shops, and airports — without sacrificing screen real estate.

### HP Premium Build
HP's signature aluminum chassis and precision engineering deliver a laptop that looks professional, feels premium, and withstands daily use.

### Wi-Fi 6E + Bluetooth 5.3
The latest wireless standards ensure fast, stable connections in modern office environments — seamless video conferencing, cloud app access, and peripheral connectivity.

### All-Day Battery
Up to 10 hours of battery life keeps you productive through a full workday without hunting for power outlets.

### HP Wolf Security
Enterprise-grade security features including TPM 2.0, HP Sure Start (self-healing BIOS), and a privacy camera shutter protect sensitive business data.

### Comprehensive Connectivity
USB-C with Power Delivery and DisplayPort alt-mode, USB-A for legacy peripherals, and HDMI 2.1 for 4K external display support — no dongle life required.
