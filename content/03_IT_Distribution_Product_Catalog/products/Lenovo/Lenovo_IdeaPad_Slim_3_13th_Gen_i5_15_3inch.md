## Key Features

### 13th Gen Intel Core i5
Intel's hybrid performance architecture combines Performance-cores and Efficient-cores — intelligent multitasking that allocates processing power where needed. Handle demanding spreadsheets, presentations, video calls, and dozens of browser tabs simultaneously.

### 15.3-Inch Spacious Display
The slightly larger 15.3" form factor provides more screen real estate than traditional 15.6" laptops thanks to slim bezels — a larger canvas for productivity without a larger body.

### Slim & Lightweight
Despite the large display, the IdeaPad Slim 3 lives up to its name — approximately 1.6 kg with a thin profile that slips easily into backpacks and laptop bags.

### Intel Iris Xe Graphics
Integrated graphics powerful enough for light photo editing, casual gaming, 4K video playback, and smooth UI rendering — no discrete GPU required.

### Dolby Audio
Dual speakers tuned with Dolby Audio deliver clear, room-filling sound for video calls, presentations, and entertainment.

### Smart Learning Features
Lenovo Smart Noise Cancelling filters background noise during calls. Smart Appearance enhances video quality — look professional in any lighting.

### Comprehensive Port Selection
Full-size HDMI, USB-C, dual USB-A, and SD card reader — connect to monitors, projectors, external drives, and peripherals without carrying adapters.
