## Key Features

### 4K UHD Camera
Crystal-clear 4K resolution ensures every participant, whiteboard detail, and presentation is visible with stunning clarity — remote attendees feel like they're in the room.

### 120° Super Wide-Angle Lens
Capture everyone in the room — even those seated at the edges. The 120° field of view eliminates the "who's talking off camera" problem common with standard webcams.

### AI Speaker Tracking
Intelligently identifies and follows the active speaker — automatically pans and frames the person talking without any manual control. Seamless, hands-free meeting flow.

### Auto-Framing
Detects the number of participants and automatically adjusts the frame — zooms in for solo presenters, widens for group discussions. Everyone is always perfectly framed.

### 5-Element Beamforming Microphone Array
Captures voices clearly up to 8 meters away with pinpoint accuracy — background noise, keyboard typing, and HVAC hum are filtered out by AI noise suppression.

### Full-Duplex Hi-Fi Speaker
Crystal-clear audio output with simultaneous talk-and-listen capability — natural conversation flow without audio cutouts or echoes.

### Universal Compatibility
Works instantly with all major conferencing platforms — just plug in the USB-C cable and select the C30R as your camera, microphone, and speaker in any app.
