### Why SSD Speed Matters for Gaming

- **Faster Level Loads** — Gen5 SSDs load game levels in under 1 second
- **No Texture Pop-In** — High throughput eliminates assets loading late
- **DirectStorage Support** — Windows 11 feature for GPU-decompressed asset streaming
- **Faster Boot Times** — From power-on to desktop in seconds
- **Quick Resume** — Switch between games without long reloads
