## Technical Support & Warranty Request

Experiencing an issue with a product purchased from Simal Technologies? Complete this form to open a support ticket. Our technical team will respond within 4 business hours.

**Before submitting, please check:**
- Is the product within its warranty period? (Check your invoice for purchase date)
- Have you performed basic troubleshooting? (Refer to the product manual or [FAQ](/resources/faq))
- Do you have your original invoice/receipt ready? (Required for warranty claims)

## Support Ticket Lifecycle

| Stage | What Happens | Timeline |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **1. Ticket Created** | Auto-confirmation email sent with ticket number (Format: TKT-YYYY-XXXXX) | Instant |
| **2. Initial Review** | Support technician reviews your ticket, attachments, and issue description | Within 4 business hours |
| **3. Diagnosis** | Technician may contact you for additional information or remote troubleshooting | 1-2 business days |
| **4. Resolution / RMA** | • If repairable: guidance or remote fix provided • If RMA needed: RMA number issued, return instructions sent • If warranty question: verified against purchase date and policy | 2-5 business days |
| **5. Closure** | Issue resolved, replacement shipped, or RMA processed. Follow-up satisfaction survey sent. | Varies by resolution type |

## RMA Process Overview

```
Support Ticket Approved for RMA
    → RMA Number Issued (via email)
    → Customer Returns Product to Simal Warehouse
        (StarSeed Technologies, JAFZA, or arrange pickup)
    → Product Inspected (physical damage, tampering check)
    → If Valid: Replacement/Repair Authorized
    → Replacement Shipped or Repair Completed
    → Ticket Closed
```

### RMA Return Shipping

| Scenario | Who Pays Return Shipping |
|----------|------------------------|
| DOA (within 7 days of delivery) | Simal Technologies |
| Valid warranty claim (within warranty period) | Customer (return to Simal); Simal (replacement shipment) |
| Invalid warranty claim (physical damage, misuse, out of warranty) | Customer (both ways, if replacement desired) |

## Not a Simal Customer?

If you purchased from a reseller and not directly from Simal Technologies:

1. **Contact the reseller first** — they are your first point of contact for warranty claims.
2. The reseller will coordinate with Simal if needed.
3. If the reseller is unresponsive, you may contact us with proof of purchase from an authorized Simal reseller.
