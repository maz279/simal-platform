## Getting to Our Dubai HQ

### By Metro

- Take the Dubai Metro Green Line to **Al Fahidi Metro Station**
- Exit the station and walk south on Khalid Bin Al Waleed Road (approximately 5 minutes)
- Dar Al Riffa Building is on your right

### By Car

- From Sheikh Zayed Road (E11): Take the Bur Dubai / Al Mankhool exit
- Follow signs toward Bur Dubai / Al Fahidi
- The building is on Khalid Bin Al Waleed Road, near the intersection with Al Mankhool Road

### By Taxi / Ride-Hailing

- Show the driver: "Dar Al Riffa Building, Khalid Bin Al Waleed Road, Bur Dubai, near Al Fahidi Metro Station"
- Approximate fares: From DXB Airport — AED 40-60; From DIC/Dubai Marina — AED 50-70

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Which location should I visit for a sales meeting?**
A: All B2B sales meetings are conducted at the Dubai HQ (Dar Al Riffa Building, Bur Dubai). Please schedule an appointment in advance.

**Q: Can I pick up my order from the retail store?**
A: Yes, if your order qualifies for retail pickup. Coordinate with your sales representative to arrange a pickup from Stellent Technologies in Bur Dubai.

**Q: How do I return a product under warranty?**
A: After receiving an RMA number from our support team, return the product to the JAFZA warehouse (StarSeed Technologies). Do not send returns to the Bur Dubai office.

**Q: Are all four locations open on Saturdays?**
A: Dubai HQ is open 9 AM — 3 PM on Saturdays. The warehouse is open 8 AM — 2 PM. The retail store is open regular hours (10 AM — 8 PM). The Dhaka office is closed Friday-Saturday (Bangladesh weekend).

**Q: Can I visit the Dhaka office for an ERP demo?**
A: ERP demos are typically conducted virtually. For Bangladesh-based inquiries, email info@simalme.com and our team will coordinate.
