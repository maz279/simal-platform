## Sales Inquiry — Request Pricing & Availability

Looking to purchase IT hardware from Simal Technologies? Complete this form and our sales team will respond with pricing, stock availability, and order options within 4 business hours during working days.

This form is for **B2B customers** — resellers, system integrators, VARs, corporate IT departments, and government entities. If you're an individual consumer, please use our [General Inquiry](/contact) form.

## What Happens After You Submit

| Step | Action | Timeline |
|------|--------|---------|
| **1. Auto-Confirmation** | Immediate email acknowledging receipt with a reference number | Instant |
| **2. Sales Review** | Sales team reviews your inquiry — checks stock, pricing, and availability against your requirements | Within 2 hours |
| **3. Response** | Sales representative contacts you via your preferred method with pricing, stock status, and next steps | Within 4 business hours |
| **4. Quotation** | If needed, a formal quotation (PDF) is prepared and sent for your review | Within 1 business day |
| **5. Order Processing** | Once confirmed, your order enters our fulfillment pipeline | Per agreed timeline |
