## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do you sell directly to consumers (B2C)?**
A: Simal Technologies is primarily a B2B distributor serving resellers, system integrators, and businesses. However, select products may be available through our retail partner Stellent Technologies in Bur Dubai.

**Q: Can I visit your office without an appointment?**
A: We recommend scheduling an appointment for dedicated attention. Walk-in visitors are welcome, but availability of specific team members cannot be guaranteed.

**Q: What languages does your team speak?**
A: English (primary business language), Arabic, Hindi, Urdu, Bengali, Tagalog, and more — reflecting our multicultural team.

**Q: Can I pick up my order directly?**
A: Order pickup is available from our warehouse (StarSeed Technologies, JAFZA) by prior arrangement. Please coordinate with your sales representative.

**Q: Are you hiring?**
A: We regularly post openings on our [Careers page](/careers). You can also submit your CV to our talent pool.
