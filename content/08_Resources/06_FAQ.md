## General Questions

**Q: What does Simal Technologies do?**
Simal Technologies Middle East LLC is an authorized IT distributor based in Dubai, UAE, with operations in Bangladesh. We distribute IT hardware from 22+ global brands and provide UniERP software solutions (built on Odoo 19 CE) through our technology partner, UniSoft Systems.

**Q: Where are you located?**
Head office: Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Branch office: Dhaka, Bangladesh. We serve customers across the GCC, Bangladesh, and select markets in Africa and South Asia.

**Q: Are you an authorized distributor?**
Yes. We hold authorized distributor certifications from all 22 brands in our portfolio. We can provide authorization letters for tender submissions.

**Q: Do you sell to end-users or only to businesses?**
We are a B2B distributor. We sell to resellers, system integrators, enterprises, government entities, and educational institutions. We do not sell directly to individual consumers.

**Q: How long has Simal been in business?**
Over 20 years. We have been serving the Middle East IT market since the early 2000s.

**Q: What markets do you serve?**
Primary: UAE, GCC (Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain), Bangladesh. Secondary: select markets in Africa and South Asia.

**Q: What is the Simal-UniSoft partnership?**
Simal provides regional presence, sales, client relationships, and project management. UniSoft Systems (Bangladesh) provides the 40+ engineer development team that builds and supports UniERP. Together, we deliver ERP solutions across the GCC and Bangladesh.

**Q: How do I become a Simal reseller partner?**
Visit [Become a Reseller ?](/partners/apply) and complete the application form. Approval typically takes 2-3 weeks.

**Q: Do you have a physical showroom?**
Our Dubai office has a product experience center where partners can see and test products. Visits by appointment.

**Q: What are your business hours?**
Sunday–Thursday, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM GST (UAE). Bangladesh office: Sunday–Thursday, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM BST.

100% genuine. Every product comes directly from the manufacturer with full warranty. We never deal in grey market, parallel import, or refurbished goods (unless explicitly stated as manufacturer-refurbished).

**Q: What is your SSD and RAM product range?**
We stock consumer, prosumer, and enterprise SSDs (SATA, NVMe, portable) and RAM (DDR4, DDR5) from Samsung, WD, Kingston, Crucial, Hikvision, SanDisk, and TEAMGROUP.

**Q: Do you sell laptops?**
Yes. We distribute Dell, HP, and Lenovo business laptops. Contact your account manager for current models and pricing.

**Q: Do you sell gaming products?**
Yes. We distribute gaming SSDs, RAM, graphics cards (Zotac, MSI, ARKTEK), gaming monitors (Koorui), and gaming accessories (UGREEN).

**Q: Do you sell networking equipment?**
We distribute Honeywell surge protectors and UPS, and can supply networking infrastructure through our brand partnerships. Contact us for specific requirements.

**Q: What is UniERP?**
UniERP is a comprehensive ERP system built on Odoo 19 Community Edition, developed and supported by UniSoft Systems. It includes modules for finance, HR, sales, inventory, manufacturing, and industry-specific solutions for 12+ verticals.

**Q: Is UniERP different from standard Odoo?**
Yes. UniERP includes significant enhancements beyond standard Odoo CE: UAE/Bangladesh localization (VAT, WPS, government pay scales), industry-specific modules (RMG, Pharma, Healthcare, Government), UniVAT (NBR-approved VAT module for Bangladesh), and dedicated support.

UniVAT is an NBR (National Board of Revenue, Bangladesh) approved VAT management module integrated into UniERP. It handles VAT challan generation, input-output VAT tracking, and NBR return submission.

## B2B Portal

**Q: What is the Partner Portal?**
The Simal Partner Portal is a secure B2B platform where authorized resellers can check real-time pricing and stock, place orders, track shipments, download invoices, access marketing assets, and submit support tickets. Available 24/7.

**Q: Who can access the Partner Portal?**
Only employees of authorized Simal partners (Authorized, Premier, or Elite tier). Registration is verified against our partner database.

**Q: How do I get portal access?**
After partnership approval, you'll receive a welcome email with your Partner ID and registration instructions. If you're an existing partner and need access, contact your account manager or partners@simalme.com.

**Q: Is the pricing on the portal live?**
Yes. Pricing reflects your partner tier, current promotions, and real-time stock availability.

**Q: Can I place orders outside business hours?**

**Q: Can I track my order status?**
Yes. The portal shows real-time order status: Confirmed ? Picking ? Dispatched ? Delivered. Tracking numbers and POD are available once dispatched.

**Q: Is the portal secure?**
Yes. All connections use TLS 1.3 encryption. We support two-factor authentication (2FA). Premier and Elite partners are required to use 2FA.

**Q: What if I forget my portal password?**
Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. A reset link will be sent to your registered email.

## UniERP (ERP Software)

**Q: How much does UniERP cost?**
UniERP has three pricing tiers. Starter (from $8,400/year), Professional (from $20,400/year), Enterprise (from $48,000/year). See [Pricing ?](/erp/pricing) for full details. No per-user fees.

**Q: How long does implementation take?**
8–10 weeks for Starter, 12–16 weeks for Professional, 18–24 weeks for Enterprise. Pharma deployments add ~4 weeks for validation.

**Q: Can UniERP be customized?**
Yes. Configuration is included. Custom development is available for specific requirements beyond standard UniERP capabilities.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
Yes. Data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Role-based access control. Audit trail for all transactions. Hosted on AWS/Azure with 99.9% uptime SLA.

**Q: Can I host UniERP on my own servers?**
Yes. On-premise deployment is available for Professional and Enterprise tiers.

**Q: Does UniERP handle UAE VAT?**
Yes. UniERP includes UAE VAT configuration (5% standard rate, zero-rated, exempt categories, reverse charge mechanism). VAT return (Form 201) data can be exported.

**Q: Does UniERP handle Bangladesh VAT?**
Yes. The UniVAT module is NBR-approved and handles all Bangladesh VAT requirements: Mushak 6.3 (VAT challan), Mushak 9.1 (VAT return), input-output VAT tracking.

**Q: Can I migrate data from my existing ERP (Tally, SAP, QuickBooks)?**
Yes. We provide data migration templates and can build ETL scripts for complex migrations. Data migration is included in implementation scope.

**Q: Is training provided?**
Yes. Role-based training is included during implementation (train-the-trainer + end-user training). Additional training packages are available.

**Q: What support do I get after go-live?**
2-week hypercare (on-site/remote immediate support). Then ongoing support per your tier's SLA: Starter (email + ticket), Professional (+ phone), Enterprise (priority 24/7).

## Warranty & Returns

**Q: How does warranty work?**
All products carry manufacturer warranty. For warranty claims, contact our support team. We facilitate the RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization) process with the manufacturer. Typical turnaround: 7–14 business days.

**Q: What is your return policy?**
Dead-on-arrival (DOA) products: return within 7 days for replacement. Change of mind: return within 14 days (unopened, subject to restocking fee). Custom-configured or special-order items are non-returnable.

**Q: How do I initiate a warranty claim?**
Submit a support ticket via the Partner Portal or email support@simalme.com with the product serial number, invoice number, and description of the issue.

**Q: What is not covered by warranty?**
Physical damage, liquid damage, unauthorized repair/modification, use outside specifications, removed/altered serial numbers.

**Q: Do you offer extended warranty?**
Extended warranty and on-site service plans are available for enterprise customers. Contact your account manager for options.
