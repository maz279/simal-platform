#### DDR5 Adoption in Enterprise — Slower Than Expected, Accelerating in H2
**📅 April 15, 2026**

Despite being available for over two years, DDR5 adoption in enterprise servers and workstations has been slower than anticipated due to platform compatibility and cost.
