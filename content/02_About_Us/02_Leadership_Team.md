## Our Leadership

Simal Technologies is guided by a visionary leadership team with decades of combined experience in IT distribution, technology solutions, and business management. Our leaders drive the strategic direction of the company, ensuring we continue to deliver exceptional value to our customers and partners across the Middle East, Africa, CIS, and GCC regions.

- **Career Development** — Clear growth paths and promotion opportunities
- **Collaborative Culture** — Cross-functional teamwork and knowledge sharing
- **Work-Life Balance** — Supportive work environment with competitive benefits
