## About Simal Technologies

### 20+ Years of IT Distribution Excellence

**Simal Technologies Middle East LLC** is a premier IT distributor headquartered in **Dubai, United Arab Emirates**. Founded in **2002** with the goal of establishing a robust distribution business in the UAE, Simal Technologies has grown into one of the most trusted IT distributor hubs in the region.

With extensive market reach spanning the **Middle East, Africa, CIS, and GCC countries**, Simal Technologies has built strong partnerships with System Integrators, Value-Added Resellers (VARs), Consultants, and Contractors across multiple industries.

## Our Dual Business Model

### Pillar 1: IT Distribution (Established — 20+ Years)

As an **authorized IT distributor** for 20+ global brands, we provide a complete portfolio of IT hardware and accessories:

- Enterprise SSDs and RAM (Crucial, HIKVISION, Samsung, Kingston, WD, SanDisk)
- NAS storage solutions and portable SSDs
- Docking stations, hubs, and adapters (UGREEN)
- Networking devices and cables
- Graphics cards (ARKTEK, Zotac, PNY)
- Surge protectors and power solutions (Honeywell)
- Monitors (Koorui, Aiwa)
- Laptops and notebooks (Dell, HP, Lenovo)
- AV and meeting room solutions (Nearity)

We serve **retailers, resellers, e-commerce platforms** (Amazon, Sharaf DG, Microless), **System Integrators, VARs, Consultants, and Contractors** through a B2B inquiry-based model with personalized pricing and dedicated account support.

### Pillar 2: Software & ERP Solutions (New Division)

Through our strategic partnership with **UniSoft Systems Limited**, we now offer comprehensive enterprise software solutions:

- **UniERP** — Full-range ERP system based on Odoo 19 Community Edition
- **Custom Software Development** — Web applications, mobile apps, API development
- **IT Consulting** — Digital transformation advisory and system integration
- **Managed Services** — AMC, cloud services, security management

**UniSoft Systems Limited** brings 40+ software engineers, 150+ completed projects, 98% on-time delivery, and is one of only 5 companies in Bangladesh with NBR-approved VAT software (UniVAT).
