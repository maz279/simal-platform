## Our Mission

## Core Competencies

Simal Technologies has developed six key competencies that form the foundation of our competitive advantage:

### 1. Enterprise-Level Networking Solutions
Designing and delivering comprehensive networking infrastructure for businesses of all sizes — from structured cabling to enterprise-grade switches, routers, and wireless solutions.

### 2. Security Solutions
Providing end-to-end security ecosystems including surveillance systems (HIKVISION), firewall solutions (Sophos XG Series), cloud security, and access control — protecting businesses from physical and cyber threats.

### 3. Advanced Data Storage
Offering the complete spectrum of storage solutions — from consumer SSDs to enterprise NAS/SAN systems, data recovery services, and backup architecture — ensuring data availability and business continuity.

### 4. Computing Hardware Distribution
Supplying the full range of computing hardware — desktops, laptops, servers, graphics cards, motherboards, memory modules — from the world's most trusted brands.

### 5. Technical Expertise & Support
Delivering pre-sales consultation, solution architecture, installation support, and after-sales service through our team of certified technical professionals.

### 6. Customer Satisfaction Focus
Maintaining a customer-first approach that has earned us a 4.5/5 average customer satisfaction rating, repeat business, and long-term partnerships across the region.
