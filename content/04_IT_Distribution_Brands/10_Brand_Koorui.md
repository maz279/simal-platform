## Why Choose KOORUI?

### Gaming-First Design
Every KOORUI monitor is purpose-built for gaming — not repurposed office displays. From high refresh rates to adaptive sync and low response times, the specs prioritize your gameplay.

### Powered by HKC
Backed by HKC's manufacturing scale — one of the largest LCD panel producers globally. This vertical integration ensures consistent panel quality and competitive pricing.

### Fast IPS Technology
The G2711P uses Fast IPS panels that combine IPS color accuracy and viewing angles with gaming-grade response times — no compromise between visuals and performance.

### 1000R Curvature (34" Model)
The deepest curve available matches the natural curvature of the human eye — maximum immersion for sim racing, flight sims, and cinematic RPGs.

### Adaptive-Sync Across All Models
Every KOORUI monitor supports variable refresh rate — compatible with both AMD FreeSync and NVIDIA G-Sync for tear-free gameplay regardless of GPU brand.

### PIP/PBP Multitasking (34" Model)
Picture-in-Picture and Picture-by-Picture modes display two input sources simultaneously — game on one side, strategy guide or Discord on the other.

### 1ms MPRT Response
Near-instant pixel response eliminates ghosting and motion blur — critical for tracking fast-moving targets in competitive shooters.
