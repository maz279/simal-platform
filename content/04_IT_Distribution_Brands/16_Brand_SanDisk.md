## Brand Story

Founded in 1988 in Silicon Valley, SanDisk pioneered flash storage technology — inventing the CompactFlash card and later the SD card standard that transformed digital photography. In 2016, SanDisk was acquired by Western Digital, combining SanDisk's flash expertise with WD's storage engineering legacy.
