## Why Choose Nearity?

### One Device. Complete Solution.
Camera, microphone array, and speaker — all in one USB-powered bar. No complicated wiring, no separate components, no AV specialist required for setup.

### 4K UHD Video Quality
Crystal-clear 4K resolution captures every participant, whiteboard detail, and presentation with stunning clarity. Remote attendees feel genuinely present in the room.

### 120° Super Wide-Angle Lens
Captures everyone seated at the table — even those at the far edges. Eliminates the "who's speaking off camera" problem common with narrow webcams.

### AI-Powered Intelligence
- **Speaker Tracking** — Camera automatically follows the active speaker
- **Auto-Framing** — Adjusts zoom to perfectly frame all participants
- **Close-Up Group** — Focuses on subsets when contextually relevant

### 5-Element Beamforming Microphone Array
Captures voices clearly up to 8 meters (26 feet) with pinpoint accuracy. AI noise suppression filters out keyboard typing, HVAC hum, and background chatter.

### Full-Duplex Hi-Fi Audio
Crystal-clear speaker output with simultaneous talk-and-listen capability — conversations flow naturally without audio cutouts, echoes, or feedback.

### Universal Plug-and-Play
USB-C connectivity works instantly with Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Webex, Skype, and any other conferencing platform. No drivers, no software, no IT configuration.
