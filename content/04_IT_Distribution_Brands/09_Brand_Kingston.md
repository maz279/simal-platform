## Brand Story

Founded in 1987 by John Tu and David Sun in Fountain Valley, California, Kingston began as a memory module manufacturer during the PC revolution. Today, Kingston is:

- **World's #1** memory module manufacturer (DRAM revenue)
- **World's #2** SSD manufacturer (channel market)
- Over **100 million** products shipped annually
- Products in over **175 countries**
- **$15+ billion** in cumulative revenue

Kingston owns and operates its manufacturing facilities, ensuring end-to-end quality control from component sourcing to final testing — a rarity in the memory industry.
