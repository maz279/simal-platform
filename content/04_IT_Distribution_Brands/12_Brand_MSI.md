## Why Choose MSI?

### Military Class Components
MSI's Military Class certification means every critical component — capacitors, chokes, MOSFETs — meets MIL-STD-810G durability standards. Built to survive extreme temperatures, humidity, and electrical stress.

### Core Boost Technology
Optimized power delivery with digital PWM controller and premium layout — stable, clean power to the CPU for reliable 24/7 operation in business environments.

### DDR4 Boost
Isolated memory circuitry with optimized trace routing ensures maximum memory compatibility and stability — even at overclocked speeds up to 4133 MHz (OC).

### Triple Display Outputs
Simultaneous HDMI, DVI-D, and VGA ports support up to three monitors — ideal for productivity workstations, trading desks, and digital signage without a discrete GPU.

### M.2 PCIe Gen3 ×4
High-speed NVMe storage support via dedicated M.2 slot — install a fast boot drive for rapid system startup and application loading.

### Micro-ATX Versatility
Compact mATX form factor fits in standard ATX cases and compact mATX chassis — flexible for office desktops, home theater PCs, and budget gaming rigs.

### AM4 Platform Longevity
The B450 chipset supports AMD Ryzen processors from 1st Gen through Ryzen 5000 series — upgrade paths spanning five generations of AMD CPUs.

### Gigabit LAN with LAN Manager
Reliable Realtek Gigabit Ethernet with MSI's LAN Manager — prioritize bandwidth for critical applications and reduce latency for online gaming.
