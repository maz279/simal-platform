## Brand Story

Founded in 1984 in Beijing as Legend Computers, Lenovo rose from a small startup to a global technology powerhouse. The acquisition of IBM's PC division in 2005 — including the iconic ThinkPad brand — catalyzed Lenovo's transformation into the world's #1 PC company.

Today, Lenovo is a Fortune Global 500 company with:
- **$62+ billion** in annual revenue
- **77,000+ employees** worldwide
