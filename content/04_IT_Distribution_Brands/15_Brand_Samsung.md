## Why Choose Samsung?

### World's #1 Flash Manufacturer
Samsung designs and manufactures every component — NAND, DRAM, and controller — in its own fabs. No other SSD brand has this level of vertical integration and quality control.

### Inventor of 3D V-NAND
Samsung pioneered 3D vertical NAND technology, which stacks memory cells vertically — enabling higher capacities, faster speeds, and better endurance than traditional planar NAND.

### IP65 Water & Dust Resistant (T7 Shield)
Certified protection against dust and water — the T7 Shield operates reliably in rain, on dusty construction sites, and in industrial environments where standard SSDs fail.

### 3-Meter Drop Protection
Withstands drops from up to 3 meters (nearly 10 feet) — engineered for field professionals working at height, on scaffolding, and in outdoor locations.

### AES 256-Bit Hardware Encryption
Military-grade encryption protects sensitive corporate data, client information, and personal files. Optional password protection via Samsung Magician software without performance degradation.

### Dynamic Thermal Guard
Built-in thermal management prevents overheating during sustained large file transfers — consistent peak performance without throttling.

### Samsung Magician Software
Free management suite for drive health monitoring, performance benchmarking, firmware updates, and secure erase — keep your drive at peak performance.
