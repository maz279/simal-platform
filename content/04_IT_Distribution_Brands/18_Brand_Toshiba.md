## Key Technologies

### Helium-Sealed Design
Toshiba's high-capacity enterprise drives use helium-sealed enclosures, reducing internal drag and enabling up to 10 platters in a standard 3.5-inch form factor — achieving capacities up to 22TB.

### Energy-Assisted Magnetic Recording
Advanced write head technology enables higher areal density, increasing per-platter capacity without sacrificing reliability.

### Toshiba Storage Security Software
Canvio Advance and select enterprise models include password-protection and 256-bit AES hardware encryption to safeguard sensitive data.

### Ramp Load Technology
Minimizes head-disk contact during non-operation, reducing wear and improving long-term reliability.
