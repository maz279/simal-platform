## Brand Story

Founded in 1985 in New Jersey, USA, PNY has grown from a memory module manufacturer to a diversified technology company with products sold in over 50 countries. PNY is one of NVIDIA's longest-standing authorized board partners — manufacturing GeForce gaming cards, Quadro professional GPUs, and NVIDIA data center solutions.

PNY's portfolio spans:
- NVIDIA GeForce & Quadro graphics cards
- XLR8 Gaming SSDs and memory
- PRO Elite professional storage
- USB flash drives and memory cards
