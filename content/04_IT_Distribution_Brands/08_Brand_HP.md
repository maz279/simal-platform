### Premium 2K Display
The 14" 2K (2560×1440) display delivers 1.8× the pixels of Full HD — razor-sharp text, detailed visuals, and more usable workspace for productivity. An IPS panel ensures wide viewing angles and color accuracy.

### Ultraportable Engineering
At approximately 1.4 kg with an ultra-slim profile, HP laptops are built for the mobile professional — boardroom to airport lounge without compromise.

### HP Wolf Security
Enterprise-grade, hardware-enforced security including:
- **HP Sure Start** — Self-healing BIOS that recovers from attacks automatically
- **TPM 2.0** — Hardware-based encryption
- **Privacy Camera Shutter** — Physical camera block
- **HP Sure Sense** — AI-powered malware protection

Up to 10 hours of battery keeps you productive from morning meetings through evening presentations — no power outlet hunting required.
