# 01 Meta Titles — All Pages (EN/AR/FR/RU)

> **Source:** CMS-managed per-page SEO field + global template rules
> **Optimization Target:** Unique 50–60 characters per title; primary keyword first; brand suffix on sub-pages
> **Last Updated:** May 2, 2026

---

## Meta Title Rules & Conventions

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| **Unique per page** | Every page must have a distinct title — no duplicates |
| **Length** | 50–60 characters (including spaces). Google displays ~580px width (≈60 chars) |
| **Primary Keyword First** | "SSD Storage Solutions — Simal Technologies" (not "Simal Technologies — SSD Storage") |
| **Brand Suffix** | All sub-pages: `... | Simal Technologies`. Homepage: no suffix needed |
| **Separator** | Em dash (—) as primary separator; pipe (|) for suffix |
| **No ALL CAPS** | Sentence Case or Title Case only |
| **No Keyword Stuffing** | Maximum 2 keyword phrases per title |
| **CTR Optimization** | Include power words: Best, Premium, Authorized, Official, Certified, Top |
| **Local SEO** | Include "Dubai" and/or "UAE" for location-relevant pages |
| **Language-Specific** | Each language has translated titles (not just transliterated) |

## Meta Title Validation Rules

| Check | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Uniqueness** | No duplicate titles across any two pages |
| **Length** | 50–60 characters (exception: homepage ≤ 80) |
| **Brand Suffix** | All sub-pages must have `... | Simal Technologies` suffix |
| **No Truncation** | Title must display completely in Google SERP without "..." (test at 580px width) |
| **Keyword Placement** | Primary keyword within first 35 characters |
| **No Special Characters** | Avoid: – (use — em dash), "", <> |
| **Language Match** | Title language must match page `lang` attribute |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why does the homepage title exceed 60 characters?**
A: Homepage titles can be slightly longer (up to 80 characters) because they represent the entire website. Google sometimes displays a longer title for the homepage. However, the primary keyword "IT Hardware Distribution" appears within the first 35 characters.

**Q: Should I include "Dubai" or "UAE" in every title?**
A: Include "Dubai" or "UAE" in titles where local SEO is relevant (About, Contact, Product pages targeting Middle East customers). Blog posts and generic resource pages can omit location.

**Q: How are meta titles updated when content changes?**
A: Meta titles are managed per-page in the CMS. When a page's H1 or primary content changes, the SEO team reviews and updates the meta title within the same content update workflow. Automated checks flag titles that no longer match page content.
