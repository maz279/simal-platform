## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can UniERP handle both formula-based (pharma) and BOM-based (RMG) manufacturing in the same instance?**
Yes. UniERP supports mixed-mode manufacturing. You can run pharma batches, RMG production orders, and food processing work orders concurrently — each with its sub-sector-specific screens, validations, and reports. Financial consolidation happens at the legal entity level as normal.

**Q: How does the system handle subcontract challan tracking for RMG?**
Each subcontract PO generates a digital challan with unique ID. When goods return from the subcontractor, the system matches the challan quantity against received quantity and flags discrepancies. Subcontractor-wise WIP and pending challan reports are available in real time.

**Q: Is the system validated for pharmaceutical GMP environments?**
UniERP is designed following GMP principles and supports installation qualification (IQ), operational qualification (OQ), and performance qualification (PQ) protocols. A validation package (test scripts, traceability matrix, deviation log) is available as an add-on service.

**Q: Can we integrate existing weighing scales, barcode scanners, and temperature sensors?**
Yes. UniERP supports integration with industrial weighing scales (RS-232), handheld barcode/RFID scanners, temperature loggers (via IoT gateway), and label printers (Zebra, TSC, SATO) out of the box.
