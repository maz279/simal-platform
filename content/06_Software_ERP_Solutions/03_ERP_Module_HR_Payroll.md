## Manage Your Most Valuable Asset — Your People.

UniERP's **HR & Payroll module** streamlines every aspect of human resource management — from recruitment and onboarding to attendance, leave, payroll processing, and performance management. Fully compliant with **UAE Labor Law** and integrated with the **UAE Wage Protection System (WPS)**, it's the complete HRMS your organization needs.

---

## Core Capabilities

### Employee Database

- **Centralized Records** — Complete employee profiles: personal info, contact, documents, contracts, salary history
- **Document Management** — Store passports, visas, Emirates ID, labor cards, offer letters, and contracts with expiry alerts
- **Organizational Chart** — Dynamic org chart reflecting reporting structures in real time
- **Employee Self-Service Portal** — Employees update personal info, download payslips, apply for leave
- **Role-Based Access** — Granular access controls for HR, managers, and employees

### Recruitment & Onboarding

- **Job Positions** — Define and publish job openings with requirements and salary ranges
- **Applicant Tracking** — Manage applications through pipeline stages: Applied → Screened → Interviewed → Offered → Hired
- **Interview Scheduling** — Schedule and track interviews with automated email notifications
- **Offer Management** — Generate offer letters with salary, benefits, and joining date
- **Onboarding Checklist** — Automated onboarding tasks: IT setup, document collection, orientation scheduling

### Attendance & Time Tracking

- **Biometric Integration** — Connect with fingerprint, facial recognition, and RFID attendance devices
- **Geofencing** — Mobile check-in/out with GPS location verification for field staff
- **Overtime Calculation** — Automatic overtime calculation per UAE Labor Law rates (125% normal, 150% holiday)
- **Shift Management** — Define and assign shifts (morning, evening, night, rotating)
- **Real-Time Dashboard** — See who's in, out, late, or absent at a glance

### Leave Management

| Leave Type | UAE Labor Law Provision |
|-----------|------------------------|
| **Annual Leave** | 30 calendar days per year (after 1 year of service) |
| **Sick Leave** | 90 days per year (15 full pay, 30 half pay, 45 unpaid) |
| **Maternity Leave** | 60 days (45 full pay, 15 half pay) |
| **Paternity Leave** | 5 working days |
| **Public Holidays** | Auto-calculated per UAE holiday calendar |
| **Unpaid Leave** | Configurable with approval workflow |
| **Emergency Leave** | Configurable per company policy |

- **Leave Requests** — Submit via self-service portal or mobile; auto-routed to manager for approval
- **Leave Balances** — Real-time accrual tracking; employees see available balance before applying
- **Leave Calendar** — Team-wide visibility of who's off and when
- **Carry-Forward** — Configurable leave carry-forward policies per UAE Labor Law

### Payroll Processing

- **Salary Structures** — Define basic salary, allowances (housing, transport, phone), and deductions
- **UAE Labor Law Compliance** — Gratuity calculation (21 days/year for first 5 years, 30 days/year thereafter), end-of-service benefits
- **WPS Integration** — Generate and submit WPS (Wage Protection System) salary files for UAE Central Bank compliance
- **Multi-Currency Payroll** — Process payroll in AED with multi-currency salary definitions
- **Payslip Generation** — Auto-generated, UAE Labor Law-compliant payslips in English and Arabic
- **Bank File Generation** — Generate salary transfer files for all UAE banks
- **Airfare & Ticket Management** — Track annual airfare entitlements per employment contract
- **Loan & Advance Management** — Employee loans and salary advances with repayment schedules

### Performance Management

- **Appraisal Templates** — Configurable appraisal forms with rating scales and competency frameworks
- **360° Feedback** — Multi-source feedback from managers, peers, subordinates, and self-assessment
- **Goal Setting (OKRs)** — Align individual goals with department and company objectives
- **Performance Reviews** — Schedule and manage review cycles with automated reminders
- **Performance Reports** — Identify top performers, training needs, and performance trends

### Training & Development

- **Training Catalog** — Define and publish available training programs
- **Training Requests** — Employees request training; managers approve with budget tracking
- **Certification Tracking** — Track employee certifications with expiry alerts
- **Training Evaluation** — Post-training feedback and effectiveness measurement

## UAE Labor Law Compliance

UniERP HR & Payroll is built with UAE Labor Law embedded:

| Compliance Area | How UniERP Handles It |
|----------------|----------------------|
| **Gratuity** | Automatic end-of-service gratuity calculation per UAE Labor Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021) |
| **Working Hours** | 8 hours/day, 48 hours/week standard; reduced during Ramadan (6 hours/day) |
| **Overtime** | 125% of basic salary for daytime OT; 150% for night/weekend/holiday OT |
| **Leave Entitlements** | All UAE statutory leave types pre-configured |
| **WPS Compliance** | Salary file generation in UAE Central Bank WPS format |
| **Employment Contracts** | Limited and unlimited contract types with auto-renewal alerts |
| **Visa & Documentation** | Passport, visa, Emirates ID, labor card expiry tracking with auto-alerts |
| **Medical Insurance** | Employee and dependent insurance tracking per Dubai Health Authority requirements |

## Why UniERP HR & Payroll?

### One System, No Spreadsheets
Eliminate the chaos of multiple spreadsheets, separate payroll software, and manual leave tracking. One unified HRMS.

### Employee Self-Service
Employees handle leave requests, download payslips, and update their information — freeing HR from administrative burden.

### Integrated with Finance

No per-employee-per-month SaaS fees. The HR module is included in the UniERP platform at no license cost.
