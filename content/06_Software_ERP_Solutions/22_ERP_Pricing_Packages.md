## Implementation Cost — What Affects It?

| Factor | Impact |
|---|---|
| **Number of Modules** | Finance + HR + Sales is the baseline. Each additional module adds configuration, testing, and training effort. |
| **Company Size** | More users = more training, more UAT, more change management. But no per-user license fee. |
| **Data Migration Complexity** | Clean master data in Excel = simple. Legacy ERP with 10 years of messy transactions = complex. |
| **Integrations Required** | Standard integrations (payment gateway, SMS, email) are included. Custom API integrations with proprietary systems add cost. |
| **Custom Development** | Configuration is included. Custom code (new features not in UniERP) is billed separately. |
| **Locations** | Single location = standard. Multi-location with different tax jurisdictions, currencies, and languages adds configuration effort. |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Are there really no per-user license fees?**
Correct. UniERP is built on Odoo 19 Community Edition (open source, no license fees). You pay for implementation (one-time) and annual subscription (hosting + support + updates). Add 10 users or 1,000 — the cost doesn't change.

**Q: What's the difference between Starter and Professional for a 30-person company?**
If you need Finance + HR + Sales only, Starter works. If you want Inventory, Manufacturing, or an Industry Module (Retail POS, etc.), you need Professional. Most companies with 30+ employees benefit from Professional because they have inventory or need multi-location features.

**Q: Can we start with Starter and upgrade later?**
Yes. You can upgrade at any time. Your data, configurations, and user accounts remain the same — we simply activate additional modules. The implementation cost difference and prorated annual subscription are charged upon upgrade.

**Q: What's your payment structure?**
Implementation: 40% at contract signing, 30% at UAT completion, 30% at go-live. Annual subscription: Paid annually in advance (monthly and quarterly payment plans available with 5% and 3% surcharge, respectively).

**Q: Is there a minimum contract period?**
Annual subscription is a 12-month commitment. After the first year, it renews annually. No long-term lock-in — cancel with 90 days' notice before renewal.

**Q: Can we host UniERP on our own servers instead of cloud?**
Yes. On-premise deployment is available for Professional and Enterprise tiers. It requires a one-time setup fee and you're responsible for server hardware, OS, backup, and security. Most clients choose cloud hosting for simplicity.

**Q: Do you offer discounts for NGOs, educational institutions, or government entities?**
Yes. We offer special pricing for registered non-profits, educational institutions, and government entities. Please contact us with your registration details for a tailored quote.
