## Deliver Every Project On Time and On Budget.

UniERP's **Project Management module** gives project managers complete control — plan projects, allocate resources, track tasks, monitor budgets, and measure profitability in real time. Whether you're managing internal projects, client engagements, or construction sites, UniERP keeps everything on track.

---

## Core Capabilities

### Project Planning
- **Project Creation** — Define projects with start/end dates, milestones, budget, and team
- **Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)** — Break projects into phases, tasks, and sub-tasks
- **Gantt Charts** — Interactive drag-and-drop Gantt view for visual planning
- **Kanban Boards** — Agile task management with customizable columns
- **Critical Path** — Automatic critical path calculation to identify schedule risks

### Resource Allocation
- **Resource Planning** — Assign employees to tasks with planned hours and availability checks
- **Workload View** — See team workload at a glance; identify over/under allocation
- **Resource Pool** — Manage shared resources across multiple projects

### Task Management
- **Task Creation & Assignment** — Create tasks, assign to team members, set priorities and deadlines
- **Subtasks & Checklists** — Break complex tasks into manageable pieces
- **Task Collaboration** — Comments, mentions, file attachments, and real-time notifications

### Time Tracking
- **Timesheets** — Log time against tasks with start/stop timers or manual entry
- **Timesheet Approval** — Manager review and approval workflow for billable hours
- **Billable vs. Non-Billable** — Auto-calculate billable amounts

### Budget & Financial Management
- **Project Budget** — Define budget by cost category: labor, materials, subcontractors, expenses
- **Cost Tracking** — Real-time cost accumulation from timesheets, purchase orders, and expenses
- **Budget vs. Actual** — Live comparison with variance alerts
- **Project Invoicing** — Bill customers based on time & materials, fixed price, or milestone
- **Project Profitability** — Complete P&L per project: revenue – costs = profit margin
