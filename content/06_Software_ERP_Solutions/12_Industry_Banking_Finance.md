## Security Architecture

- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Segregation of duties — maker, checker, authorizer roles enforced at field level
- **Data Encryption:** AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit
- **IP Restriction:** Configurable by user, branch, or role
- **Session Management:** Auto-logout after inactivity, concurrent session control
- **Data Residency:** Hosted in-country (UAE, Bangladesh) per regulatory requirement
- **Penetration Testing:** Annual third-party penetration test; vulnerability disclosure program

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does UniERP replace our core banking system (CBS)?**
No. UniERP complements your CBS. It handles the non-core functions: finance/GL, procurement, HR & payroll, fixed assets, internal audit, compliance, and risk. It integrates with your CBS for GL posting and reconciliation. This is the standard architecture for most banks.

**Q: How does the system stay updated with changing regulatory requirements?**
We maintain a dedicated regulatory compliance team that tracks central bank circulars and IFRS updates. Regulatory return templates are updated and deployed through regular version upgrades. Critical regulatory changes are deployed as hotfixes within the compliance deadline.

**Q: Can the system handle multi-currency consolidation for international operations?**
Yes. UniERP supports multi-currency transactions, translation at multiple rates (closing rate, average rate, historical rate), and CTA (Cumulative Translation Adjustment) computation per IAS 21.

**Q: What about IFRS 17 for insurance contracts?**
UniERP's insurance module includes IFRS 17-ready data structures: grouping by portfolio/cohort, fulfillment cash flows (PV of future cash flows + risk adjustment), contractual service margin (CSM) computation, and disclosure templates. Full IFRS 17 compliance is delivered as part of our insurance package.
