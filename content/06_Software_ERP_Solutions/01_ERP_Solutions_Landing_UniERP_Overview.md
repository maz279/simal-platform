# UniERP — Enterprise Resource Planning. Simplified.

**UniERP** is a comprehensive, modular Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system built on **Odoo 19 Community Edition** — the world's most popular open-source ERP platform with over 12 million users worldwide. Through a strategic partnership between **Simal Technologies** and **UniSoft Systems Limited**, UniERP combines global-class ERP capabilities with deep local expertise.

## Key Differentiators

### 1. UniVAT — NBR-Approved VAT Compliance
UniVAT is a certified VAT management module approved by the **National Board of Revenue (NBR) of Bangladesh** — one of only 5 such approved systems. For businesses operating in or trading with Bangladesh, this ensures full VAT compliance out of the box, including VAT returns, input-output calculations, and mushok generation.

### 2. No License Fees
Built on Odoo 19 Community Edition, UniERP eliminates the per-user licensing costs that make traditional ERPs prohibitively expensive for SMEs. You pay only for implementation, customization, training, and optional ongoing support.

### 3. Open Source — Full Code Access
Unlike proprietary ERPs where you're locked into the vendor, UniERP gives you full access to the source code. Your IT team can extend, modify, and integrate without vendor dependency.

### 4. Localized for UAE & GCC
Multi-currency (AED, USD, EUR, and 30+ others), VAT-compliant invoicing (UAE Federal Tax Authority standards), bilingual interfaces (English/Arabic), and compliance with UAE labor law for payroll.

### 5. Rapid Implementation
Typical UniERP deployment: **4–12 weeks** (vs. 6–18 months for traditional ERP). Our agile methodology emphasizes quick wins — get core modules live fast, then expand incrementally.
