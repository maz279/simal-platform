## Complete Visibility. From Warehouse to Delivery.

UniERP's **Inventory & Supply Chain module** gives you real-time visibility and control over your entire supply chain — from procurement and warehousing to delivery. Optimize stock levels, automate reordering, manage multiple warehouses, and track every item with barcode precision.

---

## Core Capabilities

### Stock Management
- **Real-Time Inventory** — Live stock quantities across all locations, updated instantly with every transaction
- **Stock Valuation** — FIFO, LIFO, Standard, and Average Cost methods
- **Serial Number Tracking** — Track individual items by serial number throughout their lifecycle
- **Lot/Batch Tracking** — Track batches for expiry date management, recall readiness, and traceability
- **Stock Levels** — Min/max rules with automatic reorder triggers
- **Inventory Adjustments** — Cycle counts, physical inventory counts with variance reporting

### Purchase Management
- **Purchase Requisitions** — Internal requests triggering purchase orders with approval workflow
- **Request for Quotation (RFQ)** — Send RFQs to multiple suppliers; compare and select best offer
- **Purchase Orders** — Convert approved RFQs to POs; track order status in real time
- **Blanket Orders** — Long-term purchase agreements with release orders
- **3-Way Matching** — Purchase Order → Goods Receipt → Vendor Invoice matching with tolerance control
- **Landed Costs** — Allocate freight, insurance, customs, and handling to product cost

### Warehouse Management
- **Multi-Warehouse** — Manage unlimited warehouses, each with multiple zones and bin locations
- **Putaway Strategies** — Automated putaway rules: fixed location, random, zone-based
- **Picking Strategies** — FEFO (First Expiry First Out), FIFO, LIFO, batch picking, wave picking
- **Packing** — Pack items into boxes/pallets with weight and dimension tracking for shipping
- **Delivery Orders** — Generate delivery orders with carrier integration and tracking numbers
- **Returns Management** — RMA processing with quality inspection and restocking rules

### Barcode Integration
- **Barcode Scanning** — Scan items for receiving, picking, packing, shipping, and inventory counts
- **Barcode Label Printing** — Generate and print barcode labels (Code 128, QR, EAN-13, UPC)
- **Mobile Scanning** — Use smartphone cameras or dedicated barcode scanners via UniERP mobile app

### Multi-Location Inventory
- **Inter-Warehouse Transfers** — Transfer stock between warehouses with automated documentation
- **Consignment Stock** — Track stock at customer locations; invoice only when consumed
- **Drop Shipping** — Route orders directly from supplier to customer without touching your warehouse

### Supplier Management
- **Supplier Database** — Complete supplier profiles with pricing, lead times, and performance history
- **Supplier Price Lists** — Multiple price lists per supplier with validity dates
- **Supplier Evaluation** — Rate suppliers on delivery performance, quality, and pricing
- **Purchase Analytics** — Spend analysis by supplier, category, and period
