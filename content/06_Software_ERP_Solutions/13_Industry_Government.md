## Government ERP — Public Funds, Public Trust, Digital Accountability

Government entities face a unique set of demands: transparent procurement that withstands public scrutiny, budget execution tracked to the last dirham, audit trails that satisfy supreme audit institutions, and citizen services measured by speed and satisfaction. UniERP for Government delivers all of this on a secure, sovereign-grade platform.

| Government Entity | Key Challenges | UniERP Solution |
|---|---|---|
| **Ministries & Departments** | Budget allocation tracking, multi-level approval hierarchies, fund release management, parliamentary/legislative reporting | Budget formulation to execution, configurable approval matrices, fund release workflow, automated performance reports |
| **Autonomous Bodies / Regulatory Authorities** | Fee collection, license/permit issuance, inspection management, public grievance handling | Digital fee counter, license lifecycle (apply ? review ? approve ? renew), inspection scheduling & report, citizen grievance portal |
| **State-Owned Enterprises (SOEs)** | Commercial operations + public accountability, cost control, performance reporting to line ministry | Dual-mode: commercial ERP + government compliance reporting; cost center accounting, KPI dashboard for ministry |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can UniERP be deployed in an air-gapped environment for defense/security agencies?**
Yes. UniERP supports fully offline, air-gapped deployment. Updates are delivered via secure physical media with cryptographic verification. No internet connectivity is required for day-to-day operations.

**Q: Does the system support both cash-based and accrual accounting for government?**
Yes. UniERP supports cash-based accounting (standard for most governments), modified cash, and modified accrual. The system can also run dual-track (cash for budget compliance + accrual for management reporting) if required.

**Q: How does the e-GP module ensure bid confidentiality?**
Bids are encrypted at submission and can only be decrypted at the bid opening date/time using multi-key decryption (requiring at least two authorized officials). The system logs every access attempt. Bid documents are tamper-sealed with hash verification.

**Q: Is the system compliant with our national public procurement act?**
We configure the e-GP workflow to match your national PPR/PPA requirements: approval thresholds, bid publication periods, evaluation criteria, complaint window, and contract award notification. Our team has implemented government ERP in Bangladesh and UAE jurisdictions.
