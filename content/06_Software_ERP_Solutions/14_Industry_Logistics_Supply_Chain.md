## Logistics ERP — Every Shipment, Every Vehicle, Every Warehouse, One Platform

Logistics companies live and die by operational efficiency — fuel costs, fleet utilization, delivery SLA adherence, warehouse throughput, and real-time visibility. UniERP for Logistics & Supply Chain brings fleet, freight, warehousing, and customer operations into a single unified system that tracks every dollar and every delivery in real time.

| Logistics Segment | Core Challenges | UniERP Solution |
|---|---|---|
| **Trucking & Fleet** | Fuel cost control, driver management, vehicle maintenance, trip profitability, route optimization | Trip management from dispatch to settlement, fuel log with variance alerts, preventive maintenance scheduler, GPS-tracked route adherence, trip-wise P&L |
| **Freight Forwarding** | Multi-leg shipment coordination, customs documentation, container tracking, multi-currency billing | Shipment lifecycle (FCL, LCL, air, multimodal), document generation (HAWB, MAWB, BL), container tracking, auto-charge invoicing |
| **Courier & Last Mile** | High-volume parcel tracking, POD (Proof of Delivery), COD management, delivery time window compliance | Barcode-based parcel tracking, mobile app POD (signature + photo + GPS), COD collection reconciliation, route optimization with time windows |
| **Warehousing** | Inventory accuracy, put-away & picking efficiency, space utilization, billing (storage + handling) | WMS with bin-level inventory, RF scanning (put-away, pick, pack, ship), slotting optimization, 3PL billing engine (storage/day + transaction-based) |
| **3PL & Distribution** | Multi-client operations, client-specific SLA & billing, value-added services tracking | Multi-tenant warehouse, client-specific inventory ownership, activity-based billing, VAS (labeling, kitting, repacking) workflow |

| Feature | Capability |
|---|---|
| **Vehicle Master** | Vehicle register — type, capacity (ton/m³), registration, insurance, fitness certificate with renewal alerts |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can the system handle both fleet management and warehouse operations in one instance?**
Yes. Fleet, freight, courier, and warehouse are all native modules within UniERP. A company running trucks, operating warehouses, and providing courier services can manage all three in a single system with consolidated financial reporting.

**Q: Does the route optimization consider real-time traffic?**
UniERP's route optimization integrates with mapping services (Google Maps, HERE) to factor in real-time traffic, road closures, and vehicle restrictions (bridge heights, weight limits). Routes are recalculated dynamically.

**Q: How does COD reconciliation work?**
The delivery agent marks the consignment as "Delivered — COD Collected" in the mobile app with the collected amount. At day-end, the agent remits cash to the office or bank. The system matches collected amounts against COD values and flags discrepancies for investigation.

**Q: Can different 3PL clients have different billing rates?**
Yes. Each client has a configurable rate card: storage rates (per pallet, per m², per CBM), transaction rates (per receipt, per pick, per dispatch), and VAS rates. The billing engine automatically calculates charges based on activity logs and generates client-specific invoices.
