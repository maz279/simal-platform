## Turn Leads into Loyal Customers.

UniERP's **Sales & CRM module** gives your sales team everything they need to manage the complete customer lifecycle — from first contact to closed deal and beyond. Track leads, manage pipelines, generate professional quotations, process orders, and nurture customer relationships — all from a single, unified platform.

---

## Core Capabilities

### Lead Management
- **Lead Capture** — Capture leads from website forms, email, trade shows, and manual entry
- **Lead Scoring** — Automatically score leads based on engagement, demographics, and behavior
- **Lead Assignment** — Auto-assign leads to sales reps based on territory, product, or round-robin rules
- **Lead Enrichment** — Automatically enrich lead profiles with company data and social media information
- **Lead to Opportunity Conversion** — One-click conversion when a lead becomes qualified

### Opportunity Pipeline

| Stage | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **New** | Initial contact made; needs assessment pending |
| **Qualified** | Budget, authority, need, timeline (BANT) confirmed |
| **Proposal** | Solution proposed; quotation sent |
| **Negotiation** | Terms being discussed; pricing negotiated |
| **Won** | Deal closed — converted to sales order |
| **Lost** | Deal lost — with reason tracking for analysis |

- **Kanban View** — Drag-and-drop opportunities through pipeline stages
- **Pipeline Forecasting** — Predict revenue by stage, probability, and expected close date
- **Activity Tracking** — Log calls, emails, meetings, and notes against each opportunity
- **Lost Reason Analysis** — Track why deals are lost to improve win rates

### Quotation Management
- **Product Catalog Integration** — Select products directly from UniERP inventory with real-time pricing
- **Multi-Currency Quotations** — Generate quotes in customer's preferred currency
- **Quotation Templates** — Professional, branded PDF templates with company logo and terms
- **E-Signature** — Customers sign quotations electronically
- **Online Quotation Portal** — Customers view, accept, or reject quotes via a secure web portal
- **Pricing Rules** — Volume discounts, customer-specific pricing, promotional pricing

### Sales Order Processing
- **Order Creation** — Convert quotations or create orders directly
- **Order Validation** — Automatic credit check, inventory availability verification
- **Partial Deliveries** — Manage backorders and partial shipments
- **Invoicing** — Generate invoices from orders with full or partial invoicing

### Customer Database (CRM)
- **360° Customer View** — Complete history: contacts, opportunities, quotations, orders, invoices, tickets
- **Activity Timeline** — Chronological record of all interactions: calls, emails, meetings, notes
- **Customer Segmentation** — Segment by industry, size, region, deal value, purchase history

### Dashboard & Reports

| Report | Key Metrics |
|--------|------------|
| **Pipeline Analysis** | Deals by stage, expected revenue, weighted forecast |
| **Sales Performance** | Revenue by rep, team, region, period |
| **Win/Loss Analysis** | Win rate by rep, product, industry, and competitor |
| **Activity Report** | Calls, emails, meetings by rep |
| **Sales Funnel** | Conversion rates from lead to closed-won at each stage |
