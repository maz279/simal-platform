## Agriculture ERP — Cultivating Efficiency Across the Value Chain

Agriculture is no longer just about soil and seeds. Today's agribusiness spans contract farming, precision agriculture, cold chain logistics, processing, and export compliance — all competing on thin margins. UniERP for Agriculture brings the entire agri-value chain onto one platform, from farm input to export invoice.

| Agri Sub-Sector | Core Challenges | UniERP Solution |
|---|---|---|
| **Crop Farming** | Crop planning, input cost tracking, yield forecasting, contract farming settlements | Season-wise crop plan with input budget, actual vs. budget cost tracking, harvest yield recording, contract farmer ledger |
| **Dairy** | Milk collection (thousands of farmers), quality-based pricing, chilling center management, processing yield | Farmer-wise milk collection with FAT/SNF-based auto-pricing, route-wise collection planning, chilling center inventory, processing BOM |
| **Poultry** | Flock management, feed conversion ratio (FCR) tracking, bird mortality monitoring, processing yield | Flock lifecycle (DOC ? growing ? laying ? culling), daily feed/egg/bird recording, FCR dashboard, processing order with yield tracking |
| **Fisheries** | Pond/tank management, feeding schedule, water quality monitoring, harvest planning, cold chain | Pond-wise stocking & feeding log, water parameter recording, harvest batch tracking, cold storage FEFO |
| **Food Processing** | Raw material quality variance, shelf-life management, HACCP compliance, by-product handling | Receiving quality inspection, FEFO picking, HACCP CCP logging, by-product & co-product costing |
| **Horticulture / Floriculture** | Perishable inventory, grade-wise pricing, export cold chain, phytosanitary certification | Grade-wise harvest recording, FEFO cold storage, export documentation with phyto certificate tracking, airline booking integration |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can UniERP handle multiple agri sub-sectors in one instance (e.g., crop + dairy + poultry)?**
Yes. All agri sub-sectors are native UniERP modules that coexist in a single instance with unified financial consolidation. A company doing contract farming, dairy processing, and poultry can manage all three seamlessly.

**Q: How does milk collection pricing work at the village level?**
The milk collection module supports quality-based pricing: price per liter/kg is auto-calculated based on FAT % and SNF % readings from the milk analyzer. The system can integrate directly with milk analyzers (Lactoscan, Ekomilk) to capture readings digitally, eliminating manual entry and disputes.

**Q: Does the system support weighbridge integration for crop procurement?**
Yes. UniERP integrates with digital weighbridges (first weight, second weight ? net weight capture). Quality parameters (moisture, foreign matter, damaged grain) are entered at the lab, and the final payable price is auto-calculated per the contract terms.

**Q: Can the system generate phytosanitary certificates and other export documents?**
UniERP auto-generates all standard agri-export documents: phytosanitary certificate application, certificate of origin, health certificate, fumigation certificate, packing list, and commercial invoice. These are populated from shipment data — no retyping required.
