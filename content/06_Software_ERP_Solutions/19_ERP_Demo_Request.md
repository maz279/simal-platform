## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is the demo really free?**
Yes. There is absolutely no cost and no obligation. We invest in demos because we're confident in our product — and we'd rather show you than tell you.

**Q: How long is the demo?**
Standard demo: 60 minutes. If you need more time or want to go deeper into specific modules, we're happy to schedule a follow-up session at no additional cost.

**Q: Who will be on the demo?**
An ERP consultant from UniSoft Systems who specializes in your industry. They know the product inside out and can answer technical, functional, and compliance questions. A Simal Technologies account manager may also join for regional pricing and support discussions.

**Q: Can I invite my colleagues?**
Absolutely. The more stakeholders who see the demo, the better the discussion. We can host up to 50 participants on most video platforms. Just let us know in advance so we can send the right link.

**Q: Can you sign an NDA before the demo?**
Yes. If you need an NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement) before discussing your business requirements, please mention it in the Additional Notes field or email legal@simalme.com. We'll execute it before the demo.

**Q: What if I need to reschedule?**
Just click the reschedule link in your confirmation or reminder email, or reply to any of our emails. No questions asked.
