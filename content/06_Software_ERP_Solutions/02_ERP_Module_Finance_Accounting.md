## Complete Financial Control. From Day One.

UniERP's **Finance & Accounting module** provides a complete, real-time view of your financial position — from general ledger and accounts receivable/payable to multi-currency consolidation and tax compliance. Built on Odoo 19 CE and enhanced with **UniVAT** (NBR-approved VAT management), it's the financial backbone your business needs.

## Core Capabilities

### General Ledger

- **Chart of Accounts** — Fully customizable, multi-level account hierarchy
- **Journal Entries** — Manual and automated journal entries with approval workflows
- **Multi-Company Consolidation** — Consolidated financial statements across multiple legal entities
- **Fiscal Year Management** — Flexible fiscal periods with automatic year-end closing
- **Audit Trail** — Complete, immutable record of all financial transactions

### Accounts Receivable (AR)

- **Customer Invoices** — Automated invoice generation from sales orders with VAT calculation
- **Payment Terms** — Flexible payment terms (Net 30, 60, 90, custom)
- **Aging Reports** — Real-time AR aging analysis with automated dunning
- **Payment Follow-up** — Automated payment reminders at configurable intervals
- **Credit Management** — Credit limits, credit holds, and customer risk assessment

### Accounts Payable (AP)

- **Vendor Bills** — Capture and process vendor bills with automatic tax calculation
- **Payment Proposals** — Batch payment processing with approval workflows
- **Aging Analysis** — Real-time AP aging with cash flow forecasting
- **Expense Management** — Employee expense submission, approval, and reimbursement
- **GR/IR Reconciliation** — Goods receipt / invoice receipt matching

### Bank & Cash Management

- **Bank Reconciliation** — Automated reconciliation with bank statement import (CSV, OFX, CAMT)
- **Multiple Bank Accounts** — Manage accounts across multiple banks and currencies
- **Cash Forecasting** — Predictive cash flow analysis based on AR/AP and budget
- **Check Management** — Print and track checks with positive pay integration
- **Petty Cash** — Manage petty cash transactions with reconciliation

### Financial Reporting

| Report | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Balance Sheet** | Multi-company, multi-currency balance sheet with drill-down |
| **Profit & Loss (P&L)** | Revenue and expense analysis by period, department, project |
| **Cash Flow Statement** | Operating, investing, and financing cash flows |
| **Trial Balance** | Dynamic trial balance with variance analysis |
| **General Ledger Report** | Detailed GL transaction listing with filtering |
| **Tax Report** | VAT/sales tax report ready for tax authority submission |
| **Budget Variance** | Actual vs. budget comparison with variance analysis |

### Multi-Currency

- **30+ Currencies** — AED, USD, EUR, GBP, SAR, BDT, and more
- **Live Exchange Rates** — Automatic rate updates from central bank APIs
- **Currency Revaluation** — Automatic unrealized gain/loss calculation
- **Multi-Currency Invoicing** — Issue invoices in customer's currency, manage in base currency

## UniVAT — NBR-Approved VAT Management

**UniVAT** is a certified VAT module approved by the **National Board of Revenue (NBR)** of Bangladesh — one of only 5 such approved systems in the country.

| Feature | Capability |
|---------|-----------|
| **VAT Calculation** | Automatic output VAT on sales, input VAT credit on purchases |
| **Mushok Generation** | NBR-compliant Mushok 6.1, 6.2, 6.3, 6.4, 6.5, 6.6, 6.7, 6.8, 6.9, 6.10 |
| **VAT Return (9.1)** | Auto-populated monthly VAT return ready for NBR submission |
| **Input-Output Co-efficient** | Automatic calculation per NBR rules |
| **VAT Deduction at Source (VDS)** | Automatic VDS calculation and tracking |
| **Supplementary Duty** | Configurable supplementary duty by product category |
| **VAT Audit Trail** | Complete transaction history for NBR audit compliance |

> **Important:** UniVAT is essential for businesses operating in or trading with Bangladesh. For UAE-based businesses, UniERP also supports UAE Federal Tax Authority (FTA) VAT standards — 5% standard rate, zero-rated, exempt, and out-of-scope supplies.
