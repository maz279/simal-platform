## From Admissions to Alumni — One Platform, Full Student Lifecycle

Managing a modern educational institution means juggling admissions, timetables, fees, examinations, library, and parent communication — often across multiple disconnected systems. UniERP for Education brings every academic and administrative function into a single, integrated platform that grows with your institution.

| Institution Type | Pain Points | UniERP Solution |
|---|---|---|
| **Schools (K–12)** | Fee collection chaos, parent-teacher communication gaps, manual report cards, bus tracking worries | Online fee payment + auto-receipt, parent portal with real-time updates, auto-generated report cards, GPS bus tracking integrated |
| **Colleges & Universities** | Credit-hour tracking complexity, multi-department scheduling conflicts, semester-wise exam management, transcript issuance delays | Automated credit-hour calculation, centralized timetable with conflict detection, semester-grade-point automation, digital transcript with QR verification |
| **Training Centers** | Batch scheduling, attendance tracking, certificate generation, corporate billing | Cohort-wise batch management, biometric/RFID attendance, auto-certificate on completion, corporate invoice generation |
| **E-Learning Platforms** | Content delivery tracking, quiz/assignment automation, student progress analytics, payment integration | LMS module with SCORM/xAPI support, auto-graded assessments, learning analytics dashboard, integrated payment gateway |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can the system handle multiple campuses under one instance?**
Yes. UniERP for Education supports multi-campus, multi-program, multi-currency operations. Each campus can have its own fee structure, academic calendar, and reporting while consolidating at the group level.

**Q: Does it support different grading systems (GPA, percentage, letter grades)?**
Yes. The system supports configurable grading scales — GPA (4.0, 5.0, 7.0 scales), percentage, letter grades (A+ to F), and custom grading systems. Each program can use a different grading scheme.

**Q: Can parents pay fees online? What payment methods are supported?**
Yes. The parent portal and student portal include an integrated payment gateway. Supported methods: credit/debit card, bank transfer, mobile wallets, and cash-at-counter (with receipt generation). Auto-reconciliation posts payments to the student fee ledger in real time.

**Q: How does the system handle semester-based credit hour systems (universities) vs. annual systems (schools)?**
UniERP is configurable for both. University mode activates credit-hour tracking, pre-requisite checking, and CGPA calculation. School mode activates annual promotion rules, subject-wise mark sheets, and term-based reporting. Most settings are toggled per program, so you can run both modes simultaneously.
