## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does a typical custom software project take?**
Timelines vary by scope. A focused MVP or single-purpose application: 8–12 weeks. A full enterprise system with multiple modules: 4–8 months. We provide a detailed timeline after the discovery phase (typically 1–2 weeks).

**Q: Do I own the source code?**
Yes. Upon final payment, you receive full ownership of the source code, database schema, documentation, and deployment scripts. No vendor lock-in. No license fees.

**Q: What if my requirements change mid-project?**
That's expected — and our Agile methodology is designed for it. New requirements are prioritized against the backlog. We discuss impact on timeline and budget transparently before adding scope. No penalty for change.

**Q: How do you ensure quality?**
Every line of code is peer-reviewed. Automated tests (unit, integration, end-to-end) run on every commit via CI/CD. A dedicated QA team performs manual exploratory testing. You get UAT access from Sprint 1.

**Q: Can you work with our existing IT team?**
Absolutely. We frequently work as an extension of client IT teams — co-development, knowledge transfer, and eventual handover are standard practice. We can also train your team on the new system.
