## From Raw Materials to Finished Products. Effortlessly.

UniERP's **Manufacturing module** provides complete control over your production process — from planning and scheduling to execution and quality control. Manage Bills of Materials, plan material requirements, schedule work centers, track shop floor operations, and control quality at every step.

---

## Core Capabilities

### Bill of Materials (BOM)
- **Multi-Level BOM** — Define parent-child relationships across unlimited assembly levels
- **BOM Versions** — Manage engineering changes with version control and effective dates
- **Byproducts & Co-Products** — Handle production outputs beyond the primary product
- **Phantom BOMs** — Temporary intermediate assemblies consumed within the production process
- **BOM Cost Roll-Up** — Automatic cost calculation from raw materials through finished goods

### Material Requirements Planning (MRP)
- **Demand Forecasting** — Forecast-driven or order-driven replenishment planning
- **Master Production Schedule (MPS)** — Define what to produce, when, and in what quantities
- **MRP Calculation** — Automatic calculation of raw material requirements based on BOM, stock levels, lead times, and demand
- **Purchase Proposals** — Auto-generate purchase requisitions for required raw materials
- **Exception Management** — Alerts for late orders, stock shortages, and capacity constraints

### Work Order Management
- **Work Order Creation** — Auto-generate from manufacturing orders or create manually
- **Work Center Scheduling** — Schedule operations across work centers with finite capacity planning
- **Work Instructions** — Attach documents, images, and videos to each operation
- **Time Tracking** — Record actual production time vs. planned for efficiency analysis
- **Material Consumption** — Record actual material usage with variance tracking

### Quality Control
- **Quality Checks** — Define inspection points: incoming, in-process, final
- **Quality Alerts** — Automatic alerts when measurements fall outside tolerance
- **Non-Conformance Management** — Track defects, root cause analysis, corrective actions
- **Certificate of Analysis (COA)** — Generate COA documents for regulated industries

### Shop Floor Tracking
- **Production Dashboard** — Real-time view of all work orders: planned, in-progress, completed
- **Tablet/Mobile Interface** — Operators record production from the shop floor via tablet
- **Machine Integration** — Connect with PLC/SCADA systems for automated data capture (custom)

### Costing
- **Standard Costing** — Pre-defined costs for variance analysis
- **Actual Costing** — Real-time cost accumulation from actual material and labor consumption
- **Manufacturing Order Costing** — Complete cost breakdown: materials, labor, overhead per order
