## Methodology & Assumptions

All savings estimates are based on:
- **150+ UniERP implementations** across 12 industries in Bangladesh, UAE, and Saudi Arabia
- **Industry benchmarks** from Gartner, APQC, and Deloitte ERP studies
- **Conservative estimates** — actual savings in your industry may be higher

Savings are calculated by comparing:
1. **Labor cost reduction:** Time saved on manual data entry, reconciliation, reporting, and approval workflows
2. **Error reduction:** Elimination of rework from manual errors in orders, invoices, and payroll
3. **Inventory optimization:** Reduced carrying cost from better demand planning and reorder automation
4. **Process acceleration:** Faster order-to-cash, procure-to-pay, and record-to-report cycles

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How accurate are the ROI estimates?**
The calculator uses conservative industry benchmarks calibrated against 150+ actual implementations. Your actual savings depend on your current efficiency, process complexity, and adoption. Most clients report savings within ±15% of the calculator's estimate — often higher, as the calculator uses conservative figures.

**Q: What costs are included in the "UniERP Annual Cost"?**
Annual costs include: software license (Odoo 19 CE — free), UniERP module subscription, hosting/cloud infrastructure, support & maintenance, and minor updates. Implementation is a one-time cost. There are no per-user license fees.

**Q: Does the calculator account for implementation and change management costs?**
Yes. The ROI timeline includes the one-time implementation cost and shows the payback period. The 3-year and 5-year net savings figures include all implementation, license, and support costs — showing true net savings.

**Q: Can I get a more detailed, customized ROI analysis?**
Absolutely. After using the calculator, you can request a detailed ROI analysis from our ERP consulting team. We'll work with your actual financial data (under NDA if preferred) to build a comprehensive business case for your management.
