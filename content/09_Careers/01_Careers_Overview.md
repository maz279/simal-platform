## Application Process

### What to Expect

| Step | What Happens | Timeline |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **1. Application** | Submit your CV and cover letter through our careers portal | You initiate |
| **2. Initial Review** | HR screens applications for qualification match | 3-5 business days |
| **3. Phone/Video Screen** | 20-30 minute call with HR to discuss your background, salary expectations, and availability | Within 1 week of shortlisting |
| **4. Technical/Functional Interview** | 45-60 minute interview with the hiring manager and/or technical lead (may include a skills assessment for technical roles) | Within 1-2 weeks |
| **5. Final Interview** | 30-45 minute discussion with department head or senior leadership | Within 1 week |
| **6. Offer** | Verbal offer followed by written employment contract | Within 3-5 business days of final interview |
| **7. Onboarding** | Document verification, visa processing (UAE), orientation, and role-specific training | 2-4 weeks post-acceptance |

### Tips for Applicants

1. **Tailor your CV:** Highlight specific experience relevant to IT distribution, ERP, or the role you're applying for.
2. **Include certifications:** Technical certifications (Odoo, AWS, Cisco, PMP, etc.) significantly strengthen your application.
3. **Write a cover letter:** Tell us why you want to work at Simal specifically — generic cover letters are easily spotted.
4. **Prepare for competency questions:** We value real examples of how you've solved problems, handled customers, or led initiatives.
5. **Show language skills:** Clearly list your language proficiencies with levels (Native, Fluent, Intermediate, Basic).
