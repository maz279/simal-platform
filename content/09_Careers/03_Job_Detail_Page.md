## Sample Job Detail: Senior B2B Sales Executive — IT Hardware (Ref: UAE-001)

### About the Role

Simal Technologies Middle East LLC is seeking an experienced **Senior B2B Sales Executive** to drive growth in our IT hardware distribution business across the UAE and GCC markets. This is a front-line revenue generation role where you will manage existing reseller and system integrator relationships while actively developing new business opportunities. You will represent a portfolio spanning 22+ globally recognized brands — including HIKVISION, Crucial, TEAMGROUP, Koorui, UGREEN, WD, Toshiba, and more — to B2B customers ranging from SME resellers to large-scale system integrators.

If you thrive in a fast-paced technology distribution environment, have a strong network in the UAE IT channel, and are motivated by uncapped earning potential — this role is for you.

---

### Key Responsibilities

| # | Responsibility | KPI/Outcome |
|---|-------------|-----------|
| 1 | Manage and grow a portfolio of 30-50 B2B accounts (resellers, VARs, system integrators, e-commerce sellers) across UAE and GCC | Revenue growth target: 15-20% YoY per account |
| 2 | Identify and onboard new B2B customers through market research, networking, cold outreach, and trade events | 3-5 new active accounts per quarter |
| 3 | Present Simal's product portfolio to prospective clients — conduct product demonstrations, prepare quotations, negotiate pricing and payment terms | Quotation-to-order conversion rate = 25% |
| 4 | Maintain weekly sales pipeline and forecast in CRM (HubSpot), providing accurate revenue projections to management | Pipeline accuracy within ±10% |
| 5 | Collaborate with product management team on pricing strategies, stock availability, and new product launches | Monthly alignment meetings |
| 6 | Coordinate with logistics and warehouse teams to ensure on-time delivery and resolve order fulfillment issues | Order fulfillment SLA = 95% |
| 7 | Monitor competitor activity, market pricing, and industry trends — providing actionable intelligence to management | Monthly competitive intelligence report |
| 8 | Represent Simal Technologies at industry events (GITEX, GISEC, channel partner events) and brand-specific roadshows | 4-6 events per year |
| 9 | Handle customer escalations professionally, working cross-functionally to resolve issues and maintain satisfaction | Customer retention rate = 90% |
| 10 | Mentor junior sales team members, sharing market knowledge and sales techniques | Internal knowledge sharing sessions |
