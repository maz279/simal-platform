## RTL Layout Specifications

### CSS Direction

```css
html[lang="ar"] {
  direction: rtl;
  text-align: right;
}

html[lang="ar"] .flip-for-rtl {
  transform: scaleX(-1);
}
```

### Layout Mirroring Rules

| Element | LTR (EN) | RTL (AR) |
|---|---|---|
| **Logo** | Top-left | Top-right |
| **Navigation** | Left to right | Right to left |
| **Hamburger Menu** | Right side | Left side |
| **Breadcrumbs** | Home > About > Team | الفريق > عن الشركة > الرئيسية |
| **Sidebar** | Left side | Right side |
| **Form Labels** | Left-aligned | Right-aligned |
| **Input Text** | Left-to-right | Right-to-left (numbers still LTR) |
| **Icons (arrows, chevrons)** | Pointing right → | Pointing left ← |
| **Timelines** | Left-to-right | Right-to-left |
| **Cards/Grid** | Left-to-right flow | Right-to-left flow |
| **Modals** | Standard | Standard (center-positioned, unaffected) |
| **Tables** | Column 1 on left | Column 1 on right |

## Culturally Appropriate Content Adaptations

### Tone & Style Adjustments

| English Version | Arabic Adaptation | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| "Get started today!" | "ابدأ رحلتك معنا اليوم" (Start your journey with us today) | More formal, relationship-oriented |
| "We're the best in the business" | "نسعى دائماً للتميز في خدمتكم" (We always strive for excellence in serving you) | Humility valued; avoid overt self-praise |
| First-name basis | Title + First Name (e.g., "الأستاذ أحمد") | Formality expected in business |
| "Hey there!" | "مرحباً بكم" (Welcome to you — plural/respectful) | Greeting must be warm but respectful |
| Direct CTAs | Softened CTAs: "يمكنك" (you can), "ندعوك" (we invite you) | Less imperative; more invitational |

### Imagery Adaptations

| Original | Arabic Version |
|---|---|
| Images of people | Ensure modest dress; prefer images with regional representation |
| Office photos | Show gender-segregated spaces where applicable; focus on professional environment |
| Handshake photos | Avoid male-female handshake imagery (use same-gender or avoid handshake shots) |
| Food/drink | No alcohol imagery; avoid during Ramadan |
| Icons | Culturally neutral icons (no religious or culturally specific symbols unless relevant) |

### Content Removals/Additions

| Action | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Add** | Ramadan business hours notice (seasonal) |
| **Add** | Arabic testimonials from GCC clients |
| **Add** | "Made in UAE" pride messaging where relevant |
| **Add** | References to UAE Vision 2031, Dubai Economic Agenda D33 |
| **Remove/Adapt** | Any content referencing alcohol, gambling, interest (riba) — alternative phrasing for financial terms |
| **Adapt** | Insurance references — use "takaful" where appropriate |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is the Arabic version a direct word-for-word translation?**
A: No. It's a culturally adapted localization. Content is rewritten by native Arabic copywriters who understand both the IT/ERP industry and Arab business culture. Tone, idioms, and cultural references are adapted, not just translated.

**Q: Do you use Modern Standard Arabic (Fusha) or a dialect?**
A: We use Modern Standard Arabic (Fusha) for all formal/static content (pages, legal, product descriptions). For chatbot and support interactions, we use a Gulf-friendly tone that incorporates common UAE expressions.

**Q: Are product names translated?**
A: No. Brand names (HIKVISION, Crucial, TEAMGROUP) remain in English. Product model numbers remain as-is. Technical specifications use Western numerals. Only descriptive text is translated.

**Q: How is RTL handled for mixed LTR/RTL content (like product specs)?**
A: Inline elements containing English/LTR text are wrapped with `dir="ltr"` to maintain correct rendering. CSS `unicode-bidi: isolate` is applied to prevent bidirectional text corruption.

**Q: Is the Arabic site hosted on a separate domain or subdirectory?**
A: Subdirectory: `simalme.com/ar/`. This is the SEO-recommended approach for multi-language sites (consolidates domain authority).
