## Russian Language-Specific Features

| Feature | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **Plural Forms** | Russian has 3 plural forms (1, 2-4, 5+). CMS configured for correct Russian pluralization: "1 товар", "2 товара", "5 товаров" |
| **Cases** | UI labels in nominative case; prepositions trigger correct case: "В корзину" (accusative), "О компании" (prepositional) |
| **Date Format** | DD.MM.YYYY (e.g., 02.05.2026) |
| **Number Format** | Space as thousands separator: 1 250; comma as decimal: 3,14 |
| **Formal Address** | Always «Вы» (formal "you") — never «ты» (informal) in business context |
| **Currency** | Symbol after number with non-breaking space: 1 250 $ or 1 250 ₽ |
| **Phone Format** | +7 (XXX) XXX-XX-XX (Russia); +7 (XXX) XXX-XX-XX (Kazakhstan) |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why only key pages and top 30 products in Russian?**
A: Russian translation focuses on the highest-demand products and pages based on CIS market analytics. GPU, SSD, RAM, and surveillance products show the strongest demand from CIS customers. Additional products are translated based on demand.

**Q: How are payments handled for CIS customers?**
A: We accept international wire transfers (SWIFT) in USD and EUR. Due to evolving international payment regulations, please contact our sales team for the most current payment options available for your country.

**Q: Do you ship directly to CIS countries?**
A: Yes. We ship via air freight (preferred) and sea freight to major CIS hubs: Moscow, Almaty, Tashkent, and Baku. Shipping times vary from 3–7 days (air) to 20–35 days (sea). Customs clearance assistance is available for B2B orders.

**Q: Is the Russian translation formal or informal?**
A: All business communication uses formal «Вы» (vy). Informal «ты» (ty) is never used in any customer-facing content.
