## French Localization — Regional Considerations

### African Francophone Market Adaptations

| Aspect | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| **Currency Display** | Primary: EUR (€); Secondary: XOF (F CFA) / XAF (F CFA) with conversion note |
| **Phone Format** | International format: +212 (Morocco), +213 (Algeria), +216 (Tunisia), +221 (Senegal), +225 (Côte d'Ivoire) |
| **Address Format** | French format: N° Rue, Code Postal Ville, Pays |
| **Business Hours** | Local time zones noted: UTC+0 (Senegal/CIV), UTC+1 (Morocco/Tunisia/Algeria/Cameroon), UTC+2 (DRC) |
| **Shipping Info** | Africa-specific carriers: DHL Africa, Bolloré Logistics, Africa Global Logistics |
| **Payment Methods** | Mobile money references: Orange Money, MTN Mobile Money, Moov Money (where applicable for B2B) |
| **Case Studies** | Prioritize African client success stories over Middle East/Asia cases |
| **Testimonials** | Feature Francophone African client testimonials prominently |

### Tone & Style

| English | French Adaptation |
|---|---|
| "We deliver to 30+ countries" | « Nous livrons dans plus de 30 pays à travers l'Afrique, le Moyen-Orient et la CEI » |
| "Best prices guaranteed" | « Prix compétitifs garantis » (softer than "best"; French business culture prefers understatement) |
| "Contact us today!" | « Contactez-nous dès aujourd'hui » (standard formal) |
| Direct CTAs | Polite imperative: « Découvrez », « Explorez », « Contactez-nous » |
| "You" (informal/familiar) | « Vous » (formal) — NEVER « tu » in business context |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why only key pages and not the full website in French?**
A: French translation is prioritized for pages that drive B2B lead generation and sales in Francophone Africa. Technical documentation, API docs, and developer resources remain in English (industry standard). Pages with lower African-market relevance receive summary translations.

**Q: Which French variant do you use?**
A: International/Standard French (as used in business and formal communication across Francophone Africa). We avoid France-specific colloquialisms and ensure terminology is understood across all Francophone markets.

**Q: How are prices displayed for African customers?**
A: Prices are shown in EUR (€) as the primary foreign currency, with a note that XOF/XAF prices depend on the daily exchange rate. For logged-in B2B customers, prices can be displayed in their preferred currency.

**Q: Is the checkout process available in French?**
A: Yes — the entire B2B ordering flow (cart, checkout, order confirmation) is fully translated into French for Francophone customers.
