## Task 6.3-2 — hero_slides.bg resolved (F-2026-N16) — **Option A: render with strict whitelist**

- `index.astro`: `bgOf()` — relative image path (`/assets/img/x.webp`) → `url("…")
  center/cover no-repeat`; hex color or simple linear/radial gradient passes; anything
  else ('' included) falls back to the canned BG gradient. Applied in BOTH the
  server-rendered slide markup (`style="…background:<validated>"`) and the client stage
  renderer (`esc(bgOf(sl.bg) || BG[i])`) — no CMS value can inject CSS.
