Frequently asked questions

Why does Simal need this information?

Three reasons: (1) UAE e-invoicing rules require your exact legal name, address and TIN on every Peppol invoice we exchange; (2) banking details protect both parties against payment fraud; (3) KYC/AML good practice requires us to know who we trade with, including beneficial owners.

How long does review take?

Most submissions are reviewed within two business days. If a document or field needs correction, we raise a query and only the flagged items need updating — you never redo the whole form.

Is my data safe?

Documents are encrypted in transit and at rest, access is restricted to authorised Simal reviewers, every access is audit-logged, and personal data is processed under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law only for onboarding and compliance purposes.

What is a Peppol ID and do I need one?

The Peppol ID is your company’s address on the international Peppol network used for UAE e-invoicing. For UAE tax-registered companies it follows the format 0235: followed by your 15-digit TRN. If you don’t have one yet, just tell us your e-invoicing status in the form — you can add it later.

What if my licence or Emirates ID expires later?

We track expiry dates and will email you a renewal reminder before expiry, so your master data never silently goes stale.
