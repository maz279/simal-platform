## 1. Executive Mandate & Architectural Principles

During the Independent Forensic Audit (October 2026), 45 distinct contradictions were identified across legacy requirements documents and the as-built production implementation. This master baseline resolves each contradiction by grounding decisions in:
1. **Actual Commercial Scope:** Active distributor agreements and verified commercial supplier relationships in the UAE/GCC region.
2. **Statutory & Regulatory Realities:** Strict adherence to UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (PDPL), UAE Commercial Companies Law, and UAE Federal Tax Authority (FTA) Peppol E-Invoicing mandates.
3. **As-Built Architecture & High-Performance Engineering:** Astro 5 Static Site Generation (SSG), Node.js 22 LTS microservice gateway, PostgreSQL 16.x ACID relational store, integer-fils arithmetic, and memory-hard scrypt + RFC 6238 TOTP authentication.
